# Homemade Beef Stew / 家常牛肉炖锅

> **Cuisine:** American | **菜系：** 美式
> **Serves:** 10 | **份量：** 10 人份
> **Prep time:** 20 min | **准备时间：** 20 分钟
> **Cook time:** 135 min | **烹饪时间：** 135 分钟

---

## Ingredients / 食材

| Amount | Ingredient | 用量 | 食材 |
|--------|-----------|------|------|
| 3 tbsp | vegetable oil | 3 汤匙 | 植物油 |
| 2 lbs (900g) | cubed beef stew meat | 900 克 | 炖牛肉块 |
| 4 cubes | beef bouillon, crumbled | 4 块 | 牛肉汤块（压碎） |
| 4 cups | water | 4 杯 | 清水 |
| 1 tsp | dried rosemary | 1 茶匙 | 干迷迭香 |
| 1 tsp | dried parsley | 1 茶匙 | 干欧芹 |
| ½ tsp | ground black pepper | ½ 茶匙 | 黑胡椒粉 |
| 3 large | potatoes, peeled and cubed | 3 个（大） | 土豆，去皮切块 |
| 4 | carrots, cut into 1-inch pieces | 4 根 | 胡萝卜，切段 |
| 4 stalks | celery, cut into 1-inch pieces | 4 根 | 芹菜，切段 |
| 1 large | onion, chopped | 1 个（大） | 洋葱，切碎 |
| 2 tsp | cornstarch | 2 茶匙 | 玉米淀粉 |
| 2 tsp | cold water | 2 茶匙 | 冷水 |

---

## Instructions / 步骤

### English

1. Heat oil in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Add beef and cook, stirring occasionally, until well browned on all sides.
2. Dissolve the bouillon cubes in 4 cups of water and pour into the pot. Stir in rosemary, parsley, and black pepper. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 1 hour.
3. Stir in potatoes, carrots, celery, and onion.
4. In a small bowl, dissolve cornstarch in 2 teaspoons of cold water. Stir the mixture into the stew.
5. Cover and continue to simmer until beef is tender and vegetables are cooked through, about 1 more hour. Adjust seasoning to taste before serving.

### 中文

1. 在大锅或铸铁锅中以中高火加热植物油。加入牛肉块，偶尔翻拌，将各面煎至上色。
2. 将汤块溶于 4 杯清水中，倒入锅中。加入迷迭香、欧芹和黑胡椒粉，搅匀。大火煮沸后转小火，盖盖炖煮 1 小时。
3. 加入土豆、胡萝卜、芹菜和洋葱，搅拌均匀。
4. 将玉米淀粉溶于 2 茶匙冷水中，搅入锅中以增稠汤汁。
5. 盖盖继续小火炖煮约 1 小时，直至牛肉软烂、蔬菜熟透。上桌前按口味调整咸淡。

## Notes / 备注

- Browning the beef well before simmering adds deep, savory flavor — don't rush this step.
- For a richer broth, substitute 1 cup of the water with red wine or beef bone broth.
- To make a thicker stew, remove the lid for the last 15–20 minutes of cooking, or add an extra teaspoon of cornstarch dissolved in cold water.
- The longer it simmers, the more the flavors develop — reheated leftovers taste even better the next day.
- Swap beef for lamb to make a traditional Irish stew.
- Serve over biscuits or crusty bread for a heartier meal.
- 牛肉提前充分煎至上色是鲜味的关键，不要省略这一步。
- 可用 1 杯红酒或牛骨汤替代等量清水，风味更浓郁。
- 想要更浓稠的汤汁，可在最后 15–20 分钟打开锅盖收汁，或额外加入少量水淀粉。
- 炖得越久味道越好，隔夜再热后风味更佳。
- 用羊肉替代牛肉即可做成爱尔兰传统羊肉炖锅。
- 可搭配饼干或硬壳面包享用，更加饱腹。
