# Pasta Carbonara / 培根蛋黄意面

> **Cuisine:** Italian | **菜系：** 意式
> **Serves:** 2 | **份量：** 2 人份
> **Prep time:** 5 min | **准备时间：** 5 分钟
> **Cook time:** 20 min | **烹饪时间：** 20 分钟

---

## Ingredients / 食材

| Amount | Ingredient | 用量 | 食材 |
|--------|-----------|------|------|
| 200 g | spaghetti | 200 克 | 意面 |
| 120 g | bacon or pancetta, diced | 120 克 | 培根或意式培根，切丁 |
| 2 large | eggs | 2 个（大） | 鸡蛋 |
| 1 | egg yolk | 1 个 | 蛋黄 |
| 50 g | Parmesan cheese, finely grated | 50 克 | 帕马森芝士，细磨碎 |
| 2 cloves | garlic, lightly crushed | 2 瓣 | 大蒜，轻压扁 |
| 1 tbsp | olive oil | 1 汤匙 | 橄榄油 |
| ½ tsp | black pepper, coarsely ground | ½ 茶匙 | 黑胡椒，粗磨 |
| to taste | salt | 适量 | 盐 |

---

## Instructions / 步骤

### English

1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook spaghetti according to package instructions until al dente. Reserve 1 cup of pasta water before draining.
2. In a bowl, whisk together the eggs, egg yolk, and grated Parmesan. Season with black pepper and set aside.
3. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add garlic and cook for 1 minute until golden, then remove and discard.
4. Add bacon and cook for 4–5 minutes until crispy. Remove the pan from heat.
5. Add the drained pasta to the skillet and toss to coat in the bacon fat.
6. Wait 30 seconds for the pan to cool slightly, then pour in the egg-cheese mixture, tossing constantly and adding pasta water a splash at a time until a silky, creamy sauce forms. The residual heat cooks the eggs — do not return the pan to the stove or the eggs will scramble.
7. Serve immediately, topped with extra Parmesan and black pepper.

### 中文

1. 烧一大锅加盐的水，按包装说明将意面煮至弹牙（al dente）。捞面前留出 1 杯煮面水备用。
2. 将鸡蛋、蛋黄和帕马森芝士碎放入碗中搅拌均匀，加黑胡椒调味，备用。
3. 平底锅中火热橄榄油，放入大蒜炒约 1 分钟至金黄后取出丢弃。
4. 加入培根丁，煎炒 4–5 分钟至酥脆。关火，离开炉灶。
5. 将沥干的意面放入锅中，翻拌均匀，让面条裹上培根油脂。
6. 等锅稍微冷却（约 30 秒）后，倒入蛋液芝士混合物，不断翻拌，同时少量多次加入煮面水，直到形成丝滑的乳脂状酱汁。余温会将蛋液烹熟——切勿将锅放回炉灶，否则鸡蛋会炒成蛋花。
7. 立即盛盘，顶部再撒帕马森芝士和黑胡椒即可。

## Notes / 备注

- The key technique is tempering the eggs with residual heat — patience here is what separates creamy carbonara from scrambled eggs.
- 关键技巧是用余温使蛋液乳化——这一步的耐心决定了做出的是丝滑酱汁还是炒蛋。
- Pancetta is more traditional; regular streaky bacon works just as well from any supermarket.
- 意式培根更为传统，但普通超市购买的五花培根同样好用。
- Add pasta water gradually — a little goes a long way to loosen and emulsify the sauce.
- 煮面水要少量多次加入，少许即可调节浓稠度并帮助乳化。
- Leftovers do not reheat well; this dish is best eaten fresh.
- 剩余意面不易复热，建议现做现吃。
