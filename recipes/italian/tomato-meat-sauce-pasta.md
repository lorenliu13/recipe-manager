# Tomato Meat Sauce Pasta / 番茄肉酱意面

> **Cuisine:** Italian | **菜系：** 意式
> **Serves:** 2 | **份量：** 2 人份
> **Prep time:** 10 min | **准备时间：** 10 分钟
> **Cook time:** 35 min | **烹饪时间：** 35 分钟

---

## Ingredients / 食材

| Amount | Ingredient | 用量 | 食材 |
|--------|-----------|------|------|
| 200 g | spaghetti | 200 克 | 意面 |
| 250 g | ground pork or beef | 250 克 | 猪肉末或牛肉末 |
| 400 g | canned crushed tomatoes | 400 克 | 番茄罐头（碎番茄） |
| 1 medium | onion, finely diced | 1 个（中） | 洋葱，切细丁 |
| 4 cloves | garlic, minced | 4 瓣 | 大蒜，切末 |
| 2 tbsp | tomato paste | 2 汤匙 | 番茄膏 |
| 1 tsp | sugar | 1 茶匙 | 糖 |
| 1 tsp | soy sauce | 1 茶匙 | 生抽 |
| ½ tsp | salt | ½ 茶匙 | 盐 |
| ¼ tsp | black pepper | ¼ 茶匙 | 黑胡椒 |
| 2 tbsp | olive oil | 2 汤匙 | 橄榄油 |
| to taste | Parmesan cheese (optional) | 适量 | 帕马森芝士（可选） |

---

## Instructions / 步骤

### English

1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook spaghetti according to package instructions until al dente. Reserve ½ cup pasta water before draining.
2. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium heat. Add onion and cook for 3–4 minutes until softened.
3. Add garlic and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
4. Add ground meat and cook, breaking it up, until browned (about 5 minutes). Drain excess fat if needed.
5. Stir in tomato paste and cook for 1 minute.
6. Add crushed tomatoes, sugar, soy sauce, salt, and black pepper. Stir to combine.
7. Simmer on low heat for 15–20 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the sauce thickens.
8. Add a splash of reserved pasta water to loosen the sauce if too thick.
9. Toss the cooked spaghetti into the sauce and mix well. Serve topped with Parmesan if desired.

### 中文

1. 烧一大锅加盐的水，按包装说明将意面煮至弹牙（al dente）。捞面前留出半杯煮面水备用。
2. 平底锅中火热橄榄油，加入洋葱翻炒 3–4 分钟至软化。
3. 加入蒜末，炒约 1 分钟至出香味。
4. 加入肉末，边炒边压散，炒至变色（约 5 分钟）。多余油脂可倒掉。
5. 加入番茄膏翻炒 1 分钟。
6. 倒入碎番茄罐头，加糖、生抽、盐和黑胡椒，拌匀。
7. 转小火慢炖 15–20 分钟，期间偶尔搅拌，直至酱汁浓稠。
8. 若酱汁太干，加入少量煮面水调节稀稠度。
9. 将煮好的意面倒入酱汁中拌匀，盛盘后可按喜好撒上帕马森芝士。

---

## Notes / 备注

- Soy sauce adds umami depth and replaces the need for extra salt — a small trick that makes the sauce taste richer.
- 生抽提升鲜味，可减少额外加盐，让酱汁更醇厚。
- Ground pork works great here and is easier to find; beef gives a heartier flavour.
- 猪肉末超市常见且好操作；牛肉末风味更浓郁，两者均可。
- Leftover sauce keeps in the fridge for 3 days or freezes well for 1 month.
- 剩余肉酱可冷藏保存 3 天，或冷冻保存 1 个月。
