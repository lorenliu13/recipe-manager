# Italian Spaghetti Sauce with Meatballs / 意式肉丸番茄酱意面

> **Cuisine:** Italian | **菜系：** 意式
> **Serves:** 6 | **份量：** 6 人份
> **Prep time:** 20 min | **准备时间：** 20 分钟
> **Cook time:** 120 min | **烹饪时间：** 120 分钟

---

## Ingredients / 食材

**Meatballs / 肉丸**

| Amount | Ingredient | 用量 | 食材 |
|--------|-----------|------|------|
| 1 lb | lean ground beef | 450 克 | 瘦牛肉馅 |
| 1 cup | fresh bread crumbs | 1 杯 | 新鲜面包糠 |
| 1 tbsp | dried parsley | 1 汤匙 | 干欧芹 |
| 1 tbsp | grated Parmesan cheese | 1 汤匙 | 帕玛森芝士（磨碎） |
| ¼ tsp | ground black pepper | ¼ 茶匙 | 黑胡椒粉 |
| ⅛ tsp | garlic powder | ⅛ 茶匙 | 蒜粉 |
| 1 | egg, beaten | 1 个 | 鸡蛋（打散） |

**Sauce / 酱汁**

| Amount | Ingredient | 用量 | 食材 |
|--------|-----------|------|------|
| ¾ cup | chopped onion | ¾ 杯 | 洋葱（切碎） |
| 5 cloves | garlic, minced | 5 瓣 | 大蒜（切末） |
| ¼ cup | olive oil | ¼ 杯 | 橄榄油 |
| 2 cans (28 oz each) | whole peeled tomatoes | 2 罐（每罐 800 克） | 去皮整番茄 |
| 2 tsp | salt | 2 茶匙 | 盐 |
| 1 tsp | white sugar | 1 茶匙 | 白糖 |
| 1 | bay leaf | 1 片 | 香叶 |
| 1 can (6 oz) | tomato paste | 1 罐（170 克） | 番茄膏 |
| ¾ tsp | dried basil | ¾ 茶匙 | 干罗勒 |
| ½ tsp | ground black pepper | ½ 茶匙 | 黑胡椒粉 |

---

## Instructions / 步骤

### English

1. In a large bowl, combine ground beef, bread crumbs, parsley, Parmesan, ¼ tsp black pepper, garlic powder, and beaten egg. Mix well and form into 12 balls. Cover and refrigerate until needed.
2. In a large saucepan over medium heat, sauté chopped onion and minced garlic in olive oil until the onion is translucent, about 5 minutes.
3. Stir in whole peeled tomatoes, salt, sugar, and bay leaf. Break up the tomatoes with a spoon. Cover, reduce heat to low, and simmer for 90 minutes.
4. Stir in tomato paste, basil, ½ tsp black pepper, and the refrigerated meatballs. Simmer uncovered for 30 minutes more, until meatballs are cooked through.
5. Remove bay leaf. Serve hot over your favorite pasta.

### 中文

1. 将牛肉馅、面包糠、干欧芹、帕玛森芝士、¼ 茶匙黑胡椒粉、蒜粉和打散的鸡蛋放入大碗中，充分混合，捏成 12 个肉丸。加盖放入冰箱备用。
2. 中火加热大锅，倒入橄榄油，将洋葱碎和蒜末炒至洋葱透明，约 5 分钟。
3. 加入去皮整番茄、盐、白糖和香叶，用勺子将番茄压碎。盖上锅盖，转小火慢炖 90 分钟。
4. 加入番茄膏、干罗勒、½ 茶匙黑胡椒粉，再放入冰箱里的肉丸，继续小火炖煮 30 分钟，直到肉丸完全熟透。
5. 取出香叶，趁热浇在意面上即可享用。

## Notes / 备注

- Fresh bread crumbs give meatballs a softer texture than dry; tear day-old bread and pulse briefly in a food processor.
- 用新鲜面包糠比干面包糠做出的肉丸口感更嫩；可将隔夜面包撕块后用料理机略打即成。
- The sauce can be made ahead and refrigerated for up to 3 days; add meatballs during the final 30-minute reheat.
- 酱汁可提前制作，冷藏保存 3 天；回锅加热时再放入肉丸，加热 30 分钟即可。
- Add a splash of red wine with the tomatoes for extra depth.
- 加入番茄时可加少量红葡萄酒，增添风味层次。
- This recipe freezes well for up to 3 months.
- 本食谱可冷冻保存长达 3 个月。
