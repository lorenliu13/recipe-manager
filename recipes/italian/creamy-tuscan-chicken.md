# Creamy Tuscan Chicken / 托斯卡纳奶油鸡

> **Cuisine:** Italian | **菜系：** 意式
> **Serves:** 2 | **份量：** 2 人份
> **Prep time:** 10 min | **准备时间：** 10 分钟
> **Cook time:** 25 min | **烹饪时间：** 25 分钟

## Ingredients / 食材

| Amount | Ingredient | 用量 | 食材 |
|--------|-----------|------|------|
| 2 | chicken breasts, boneless skinless | 2 块 | 鸡胸肉，去骨去皮 |
| ½ tsp | salt | ½ 茶匙 | 盐 |
| ¼ tsp | black pepper | ¼ 茶匙 | 黑胡椒 |
| 1 tbsp | olive oil | 1 汤匙 | 橄榄油 |
| 4 cloves | garlic, minced | 4 瓣 | 大蒜，切末 |
| 60 g | sun-dried tomatoes in oil, drained and sliced | 60 克 | 油浸日晒番茄，沥油切丝 |
| 120 ml | chicken broth | 120 毫升 | 鸡高汤 |
| 180 ml | heavy cream | 180 毫升 | 淡奶油 |
| 80 g | baby spinach | 80 克 | 嫩菠菜 |
| 30 g | Parmesan cheese, grated | 30 克 | 帕马森芝士，磨碎 |
| 1 tsp | Italian seasoning (or dried oregano + basil) | 1 茶匙 | 意式综合香料（或干牛至+罗勒） |

---

## Instructions / 步骤

### English

1. Season chicken breasts on both sides with salt and pepper.
2. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Add chicken and cook 5–6 minutes per side until golden and cooked through (internal temp 74°C / 165°F). Transfer to a plate and tent with foil.
3. Reduce heat to medium. In the same pan, add garlic and cook for 30 seconds until fragrant.
4. Add sun-dried tomatoes and Italian seasoning; stir for 1 minute.
5. Pour in chicken broth and scrape up any browned bits. Add heavy cream and bring to a gentle simmer.
6. Stir in Parmesan and simmer for 3–4 minutes until the sauce thickens slightly.
7. Add spinach and stir until just wilted, about 1 minute.
8. Return chicken to the pan, spoon sauce over it, and simmer 1 minute to warm through. Serve with pasta, rice, or bread.

### 中文

1. 鸡胸肉两面均匀撒上盐和黑胡椒。
2. 平底锅中大火热橄榄油，放入鸡胸肉，每面煎 5–6 分钟至金黄且熟透（内部温度达 74°C）。盛出放入盘中，用锡纸松松盖住保温。
3. 转中火，锅中加入蒜末，炒约 30 秒至出香味。
4. 加入日晒番茄丝和意式综合香料，翻炒 1 分钟。
5. 倒入鸡高汤，用铲子刮起锅底焦香部分，再加入淡奶油，小火慢煮至微沸。
6. 加入帕马森芝士碎，搅拌均匀，小火炖 3–4 分钟至酱汁略微浓稠。
7. 加入嫩菠菜，翻拌约 1 分钟至菠菜刚好变软。
8. 将鸡胸肉放回锅中，用酱汁浇淋，再炖 1 分钟至热透。搭配意面、米饭或面包享用。

## Notes / 备注

- Sun-dried tomatoes (in oil, jarred) are available at most supermarkets; their sweet-tangy intensity is the flavor anchor of this dish.
- 油浸日晒番茄（罐装）在大多数超市均有售；其甜酸浓郁的风味是这道菜的核心。
- For a lighter version, substitute half the cream with extra chicken broth.
- 如想降低热量，可将一半淡奶油替换为鸡高汤。
- Pounding the chicken to even thickness (as in Chicken Piccata) also works here for faster, more even cooking.
- 将鸡胸肉敲打至均匀厚度（参照意式柠檬黄油鸡做法）同样适用，可缩短烹饪时间并使受热更均匀。
- Leftovers keep in the fridge for 3 days; sauce thickens on cooling — reheat gently with a splash of broth.
- 剩菜可冷藏保存 3 天；酱汁冷藏后会变浓稠，复热时加少量高汤，小火慢热即可。
