# Chicken Piccata / 意式柠檬黄油鸡

> **Cuisine:** Italian | **菜系：** 意式
> **Serves:** 2 | **份量：** 2 人份
> **Prep time:** 10 min | **准备时间：** 10 分钟
> **Cook time:** 20 min | **烹饪时间：** 20 分钟

## Ingredients / 食材

| Amount | Ingredient | 用量 | 食材 |
|--------|-----------|------|------|
| 2 | chicken breasts, boneless skinless | 2 块 | 鸡胸肉，去骨去皮 |
| 3 tbsp | all-purpose flour | 3 汤匙 | 普通面粉 |
| ½ tsp | salt | ½ 茶匙 | 盐 |
| ¼ tsp | black pepper | ¼ 茶匙 | 黑胡椒 |
| 3 tbsp | butter, divided | 3 汤匙 | 黄油，分次使用 |
| 1 tbsp | olive oil | 1 汤匙 | 橄榄油 |
| 3 cloves | garlic, minced | 3 瓣 | 大蒜，切末 |
| ½ cup | chicken broth | ½ 杯 | 鸡高汤 |
| 3 tbsp | fresh lemon juice (about 1 lemon) | 3 汤匙 | 新鲜柠檬汁（约 1 个柠檬） |
| 2 tbsp | capers, drained | 2 汤匙 | 酸豆，沥水 |
| 2 tbsp | fresh parsley, chopped | 2 汤匙 | 新鲜欧芹，切碎 |
| 4 slices | lemon, for serving | 4 片 | 柠檬片，摆盘用 |

---

## Instructions / 步骤

### English

1. Slice each chicken breast horizontally into 2 thin cutlets. Place between plastic wrap and pound to an even ¼-inch thickness.
2. Mix flour, salt, and pepper on a plate. Dredge each cutlet lightly in the flour, shaking off excess.
3. Heat 1 tbsp butter and the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add chicken and cook 3 minutes per side until golden. Transfer to a plate and cover loosely with foil.
4. Reduce heat to medium. Add garlic and cook for 30 seconds until fragrant.
5. Pour in chicken broth and lemon juice, scraping up any browned bits from the pan. Simmer for 2–3 minutes until the sauce reduces slightly.
6. Stir in capers and the remaining 2 tbsp butter until the butter melts and the sauce is glossy.
7. Return chicken to the pan and spoon sauce over it. Simmer 1 minute to warm through.
8. Garnish with parsley and lemon slices. Serve with pasta, rice, or crusty bread.

### 中文

1. 将每块鸡胸肉横向片成 2 片薄鸡排，放入保鲜膜中，用肉锤或擀面杖敲打至约 0.6 厘米厚度均匀。
2. 将面粉、盐和黑胡椒混合放在盘中。每片鸡排薄薄裹上面粉，抖掉多余的粉。
3. 平底锅中大火热 1 汤匙黄油和橄榄油，放入鸡排，每面煎约 3 分钟至金黄。盛出放入盘中，用锡纸松松地盖住保温。
4. 转中火，加入蒜末翻炒约 30 秒至出香味。
5. 倒入鸡高汤和柠檬汁，用铲子将锅底焦香部分刮起。小火炖 2–3 分钟，让酱汁稍微收浓。
6. 加入酸豆和剩余 2 汤匙黄油，搅拌至黄油融化、酱汁呈现光泽感。
7. 将鸡排放回锅中，用酱汁浇淋鸡排，再炖 1 分钟至鸡排热透。
8. 撒上欧芹碎，配柠檬片摆盘。可搭配意面、米饭或硬皮面包一起享用。

## Notes / 备注

- Pounding the chicken to even thickness is essential — it ensures even cooking and prevents dry edges.
- 将鸡排敲打至均匀厚度非常关键，可确保受热均匀，避免边缘变干。
- Capers are available in jars at most supermarkets; their briny, tangy flavor is the signature of piccata.
- 酸豆在大多数超市的瓶装区都能买到；其咸鲜酸爽的风味正是 piccata 的灵魂所在。
- If capers are unavailable, finely chopped green olives make a reasonable substitute.
- 若买不到酸豆，可用切碎的绿橄榄代替，风味相近。
- Leftovers keep in the fridge for 2 days; reheat gently with a splash of broth to loosen the sauce.
- 剩菜可冷藏保存 2 天；复热时加少量高汤，小火慢热以保持酱汁质感。
