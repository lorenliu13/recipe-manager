# Garlic Shrimp Aglio e Olio / 蒜香虾仁橄榄油意面

> **Cuisine:** Italian | **菜系：** 意式
> **Serves:** 2 | **份量：** 2 人份
> **Prep time:** 10 min | **准备时间：** 10 分钟
> **Cook time:** 20 min | **烹饪时间：** 20 分钟

---
![](<../../figures/Pasted image 20260607215713.png>)
## Ingredients / 食材

| Amount | Ingredient | 用量 | 食材 |
|--------|-----------|------|------|
| 200 g | spaghetti | 200 克 | 意面 |
| 250 g | shrimp, peeled and deveined | 250 克 | 鲜虾，去壳去肠线 |
| 5 cloves | garlic, thinly sliced | 5 瓣 | 大蒜，切薄片 |
| 4 tbsp | olive oil, divided | 4 汤匙 | 橄榄油，分次使用 |
| ¼ tsp | black pepper | ¼ 茶匙 | 黑胡椒 |
| ½ tsp | salt | ½ 茶匙 | 盐 |
| 2 tbsp | fresh lemon juice | 2 汤匙 | 新鲜柠檬汁 |
| 3 tbsp | fresh parsley, chopped | 3 汤匙 | 新鲜欧芹，切碎 |
| 2 tbsp | Parmesan cheese, grated (optional) | 2 汤匙 | 帕马森芝士，磨碎（可选） |

---

## Instructions / 步骤

### English

1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook spaghetti until al dente. Reserve ¾ cup pasta water before draining.
2. Season shrimp with salt and pepper. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add shrimp and cook 1–2 minutes per side until pink and just cooked through. Transfer to a plate.
3. Reduce heat to medium-low. Add remaining 3 tbsp olive oil and the sliced garlic. Cook, stirring often, for 2–3 minutes until the garlic is lightly golden — watch carefully as it burns quickly.
4. Add the drained pasta to the skillet and toss to coat. Add pasta water a splash at a time, tossing, until the pasta is glossy and lightly sauced.
5. Return shrimp to the pan. Add lemon juice and parsley, toss everything together, and taste for seasoning.
6. Serve immediately, topped with Parmesan if desired.

### 中文

1. 烧一大锅加盐的水，将意面按包装说明煮至弹牙（al dente）。捞面前留出 ¾ 杯煮面水备用。
2. 将盐和黑胡椒均匀撒在虾仁上。平底锅大火热 1 汤匙橄榄油，放入虾仁，每面煎 1–2 分钟至变粉红色、刚好熟透。盛出备用。
3. 转中小火，加入剩余 3 汤匙橄榄油和蒜片，不时翻动，炒 2–3 分钟至蒜片呈淡金黄色——需留意火候，蒜片容易焦糊。
4. 将沥干的意面加入锅中翻拌，让面条裹上蒜香油。少量多次加入煮面水，翻拌至面条呈现光泽且挂上薄薄酱汁。
5. 将虾仁放回锅中，加入柠檬汁和欧芹碎，拌匀后尝味调整咸淡。
6. 立即盛盘，可按喜好撒上帕马森芝士。

## Notes / 备注

- This is a pantry-and-fridge dish — if shrimp is in the freezer, thaw under cold running water in 10 minutes.
- 这道菜主要用冰箱常备食材制作——虾仁若存于冷冻层，用冷水冲泡约 10 分钟即可快速解冻。
- Golden (not brown) garlic is the goal; pull it from the heat as soon as color appears.
- 大蒜应炒至淡金黄色而非深棕色；一出现颜色就应控制火候，以免发苦。
- Frozen shrimp works perfectly here; just pat dry before seasoning.
- 冷冻虾仁完全可以，解冻后用厨房纸吸干水分再调味，效果更佳。
- Leftovers keep for 1 day refrigerated; reheat gently with a little olive oil.
- 剩余意面可冷藏保存 1 天，复热时加少许橄榄油，小火翻炒即可。
