# Poulet à la Moutarde / 法式芥末奶油鸡

> **Cuisine:** French | **菜系：** 法式
> **Serves:** 4 | **份量：** 4 人份
> **Prep time:** 10 min | **准备时间：** 10 分钟
> **Cook time:** 25 min | **烹饪时间：** 25 分钟

---

## Ingredients / 食材

| Amount | Ingredient | 用量 | 食材 |
|--------|-----------|------|------|
| 4 | bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs | 4 块 | 带骨带皮鸡腿 |
| 2 tbsp | Dijon mustard | 2 汤匙 | 第戎芥末酱 |
| 2 | shallots, finely minced | 2 颗 | 法式葱头，切末 |
| 3 cloves | garlic, minced | 3 瓣 | 大蒜，切末 |
| ½ cup (120 ml) | dry white wine | 120 毫升 | 干白葡萄酒 |
| ¾ cup (180 ml) | heavy cream | 180 毫升 | 淡奶油 |
| 1 tbsp | Dijon mustard (for sauce finish) | 1 汤匙 | 第戎芥末酱（收尾用） |
| 1 tbsp | olive oil | 1 汤匙 | 橄榄油 |
| to taste | salt | 适量 | 盐 |
| to taste | black pepper | 适量 | 黑胡椒 |
| 1 tbsp | fresh parsley, chopped (optional) | 1 汤匙 | 新鲜欧芹，切碎（可选） |

---

## Instructions / 步骤

### English

1. Pat the chicken thighs dry with paper towels. Season both sides generously with salt and black pepper.
2. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Place chicken thighs skin-side down and sear for 7–8 minutes without moving, until the skin is deep golden and crispy. Flip and cook for another 5 minutes. Transfer to a plate.
3. Reduce heat to medium. Add shallots to the same skillet and cook for 2 minutes until softened. Add garlic and cook for 30 seconds until fragrant.
4. Pour in the white wine, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom of the pan. Simmer for 2 minutes until reduced by half.
5. Stir in the heavy cream and 2 tablespoons of Dijon mustard. Return the chicken to the skillet, skin-side up.
6. Simmer over medium-low heat for 8–10 minutes, until the sauce has thickened and the chicken is cooked through (internal temp 74°C / 165°F).
7. Stir in the remaining 1 tablespoon of Dijon mustard off the heat. Taste and adjust seasoning. Garnish with parsley and serve.

### 中文

1. 用厨房纸将鸡腿彻底擦干，两面均匀撒上盐和黑胡椒。
2. 大火加热橄榄油，将鸡腿皮面朝下放入平底锅，不要移动，煎 7～8 分钟至鸡皮深金黄色且酥脆。翻面再煎 5 分钟，然后取出备用。
3. 转中火，在同一锅中加入葱头末，炒 2 分钟至软化。加入蒜末，翻炒 30 秒至出香味。
4. 倒入白葡萄酒，用铲子刮起锅底焦香物，煮 2 分钟至酒液减少一半。
5. 加入淡奶油和 2 汤匙第戎芥末酱搅匀，将鸡腿皮面朝上放回锅中。
6. 转中小火炖 8～10 分钟，至酱汁浓稠、鸡肉全熟（内部温度达 74°C / 165°F）。
7. 关火后拌入剩余 1 汤匙第戎芥末酱，尝味调整咸淡。撒上欧芹即可上桌。

## Notes / 备注

- Searing skin-side down without moving is the key to crispy skin — resist the urge to lift the chicken early.
- 煎皮面时不要移动鸡腿，是烤出酥脆鸡皮的关键——忍住不要提前翻面。
- Adding the final tablespoon of mustard off the heat preserves its sharp, tangy flavor.
- 关火后再加入最后一勺芥末，可以保留其辛辣清爽的风味。
- No white wine? Substitute with ½ cup chicken broth + 1 tsp white wine vinegar.
- 没有白葡萄酒？可用 120 毫升鸡汤加 1 茶匙白葡萄酒醋代替。
- Serve with steamed rice, crusty bread, or mashed potatoes to soak up the sauce.
- 搭配米饭、法棍或土豆泥，充分吸收酱汁。
- Leftovers keep in the fridge for up to 3 days. Reheat gently over low heat with a splash of cream.
- 剩余鸡腿冷藏可保存 3 天，用小火加少许淡奶油轻轻复热。
