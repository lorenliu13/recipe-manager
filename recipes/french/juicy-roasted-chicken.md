# Juicy Roasted Chicken / 多汁烤整鸡

> **Cuisine:** French-inspired / Western | **菜系：** 西式
> **Serves:** 6 | **份量：** 6 人份
> **Prep time:** 15 min | **准备时间：** 15 分钟
> **Cook time:** 1 hr | **烹饪时间：** 1 小时
> **Rest time:** 30 min | **静置时间：** 30 分钟

## Ingredients / 食材

| Amount | Ingredient | 用量 | 食材 |
|--------|-----------|------|------|
| 1 (3 lb / 1.4 kg) | whole chicken, giblets removed | 1 只（约 1.4 公斤） | 整鸡，去内脏 |
| ½ cup (115 g) | unsalted butter, softened | 115 克 | 无盐黄油，软化 |
| 1 stalk | celery, leaves removed | 1 根 | 西芹，去叶 |
| 1 tbsp | onion powder | 1 汤匙 | 洋葱粉 |
| to taste | salt | 适量 | 盐 |
| to taste | black pepper | 适量 | 黑胡椒 |

## Instructions / 步骤

### English

1. Preheat the oven to 425°F (220°C). Pat the chicken completely dry inside and out with paper towels — dry skin is the key to crispy skin.
2. Season the inside of the cavity generously with salt, pepper, and onion powder. Rub 3 tablespoons of softened butter inside the cavity.
3. Gently loosen the skin over the breasts with your fingers and push a thin layer of butter underneath.
4. Cut the celery stalk into 3–4 pieces and stuff them into the cavity.
5. Rub the remaining butter all over the outside of the chicken. Season the outside generously with salt, pepper, and onion powder.
6. Tuck the wing tips under the body to prevent burning.
7. Place the chicken breast-side up on a rack set over a roasting pan or baking sheet.
8. Roast uncovered at 425°F for 50–60 minutes, until the skin is deep golden brown and an instant-read thermometer inserted into the thickest part of the thigh (near the bone) reads 165°F (74°C).
9. Baste the chicken with the pan drippings. Tent loosely with aluminium foil and rest for 30 minutes before carving.

### 中文

1. 烤箱预热至 220°C（425°F）。用厨房纸将整鸡内外彻底擦干——皮干才能烤出酥脆的鸡皮。
2. 在鸡腔内均匀撒上盐、黑胡椒和洋葱粉，并将约 3 汤匙软化黄油涂抹在腔内。
3. 用手指轻轻将鸡胸部位的皮与肉分离，塞入一薄层黄油于皮下。
4. 将西芹切成 3～4 段，塞入鸡腔内。
5. 将剩余黄油涂抹在鸡身外表，再均匀撒上盐、黑胡椒和洋葱粉。
6. 将两侧鸡翅尖折入鸡身下方，防止烤焦。
7. 将鸡胸朝上放在烤架上，烤架置于烤盘或烤板上。
8. 放入预热好的烤箱，不盖锡纸，以 220°C 烤 50～60 分钟，直至鸡皮呈深金黄色。用即时温度计插入大腿最厚处（靠近骨头），确认温度达到 74°C（165°F）。
9. 用烤盘底部的肉汁浇淋鸡身。用铝箔纸松松盖住鸡，静置 30 分钟后再切开上桌。

## Notes / 备注

- Pat the chicken completely dry before seasoning — this is the single most important step for crispy skin.
- 腌制前务必将鸡彻底擦干，这是烤出酥皮的最关键一步。
- Butter under the skin keeps the breast meat moist and flavourful throughout the long roast.
- 皮下黄油能在长时间烘烤中保持鸡胸肉的鲜嫩多汁。
- For extra flavour, add half a lemon and 3–4 garlic cloves to the cavity alongside the celery.
- 可在西芹旁加入半个柠檬和 3～4 瓣大蒜，风味更佳。
- To make pan gravy: pour drippings into a small saucepan, whisk in 1 tbsp flour, then gradually add ½ cup chicken broth. Simmer until thickened.
- 制作肉汁酱：将烤盘底部的肉汁倒入小锅，加入 1 汤匙面粉搅匀，再缓缓加入 120 毫升鸡汤，小火煮至浓稠即可。
- Leftovers keep in the fridge for 3–4 days. Reheat in a 165°C (325°F) oven with a splash of broth to retain moisture.
- 剩余鸡肉冷藏可保存 3～4 天。复热时放入 165°C 烤箱，加少许鸡汤以保持湿润。
