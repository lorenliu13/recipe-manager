# Poulet Provençal / 普罗旺斯番茄炖鸡

> **Cuisine:** French | **菜系：** 法式
> **Serves:** 4 | **份量：** 4 人份
> **Prep time:** 10 min | **准备时间：** 10 分钟
> **Cook time:** 35 min | **烹饪时间：** 35 分钟

---

## Ingredients / 食材

| Amount | Ingredient | 用量 | 食材 |
|--------|-----------|------|------|
| 4 | bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs | 4 块 | 带骨带皮鸡腿 |
| 1 | red bell pepper, sliced | 1 个 | 红灯笼椒，切条 |
| 1 | yellow onion, sliced | 1 个 | 黄洋葱，切条 |
| 4 cloves | garlic, minced | 4 瓣 | 大蒜，切末 |
| 1 can (400 g) | diced tomatoes | 1 罐（400 克） | 番茄丁罐头 |
| ⅓ cup (60 g) | black or Kalamata olives, pitted | 60 克 | 黑橄榄或卡拉马塔橄榄，去核 |
| ½ cup (120 ml) | chicken broth | 120 毫升 | 鸡汤 |
| 1 tbsp | olive oil | 1 汤匙 | 橄榄油 |
| 1 tsp | dried thyme | 1 茶匙 | 干百里香 |
| 1 tsp | dried rosemary | 1 茶匙 | 干迷迭香 |
| 1 tsp | dried oregano | 1 茶匙 | 干牛至 |
| to taste | salt | 适量 | 盐 |
| to taste | black pepper | 适量 | 黑胡椒 |
| 1 tbsp | fresh parsley, chopped (optional) | 1 汤匙 | 新鲜欧芹，切碎（可选） |

---

## Instructions / 步骤

### English

1. Pat the chicken thighs dry with paper towels. Season both sides with salt and black pepper.
2. Heat olive oil in a large skillet or Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Add chicken skin-side down and sear for 6–7 minutes until the skin is golden and crispy. Flip and sear the other side for 3 minutes. Transfer to a plate.
3. Reduce heat to medium. Add the onion and bell pepper to the same pan and cook for 4 minutes until softened. Add garlic, thyme, rosemary, and oregano; cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
4. Pour in the diced tomatoes and chicken broth, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom of the pan. Stir in the olives.
5. Nestle the chicken thighs back into the pan, skin-side up. Bring to a simmer, then reduce heat to medium-low.
6. Cover and cook for 18–20 minutes until the chicken is cooked through (internal temp 74°C / 165°F) and the sauce has thickened. Uncover for the last 5 minutes if you prefer a thicker sauce.
7. Taste and adjust seasoning. Garnish with fresh parsley and serve.

### 中文

1. 用厨房纸将鸡腿彻底擦干，两面撒上盐和黑胡椒。
2. 中大火加热橄榄油，将鸡腿皮面朝下放入锅中，煎 6～7 分钟至鸡皮金黄酥脆。翻面再煎 3 分钟，取出备用。
3. 转中火，在同一锅中加入洋葱条和灯笼椒条，炒 4 分钟至软化。加入蒜末、干百里香、干迷迭香和干牛至，翻炒 1 分钟至出香。
4. 倒入番茄丁罐头和鸡汤，用铲子刮起锅底焦香物，再加入橄榄搅匀。
5. 将鸡腿皮面朝上放回锅中，煮至沸腾后转中小火。
6. 盖上锅盖炖 18～20 分钟，至鸡肉全熟（内部温度达 74°C / 165°F）、酱汁浓稠。最后 5 分钟可开盖，让酱汁进一步收浓。
7. 尝味调整咸淡，撒上新鲜欧芹即可上桌。

---

## Notes / 备注

- Keep the chicken skin-side up during the braise so the skin stays crispy instead of going soggy.
- 炖煮时鸡腿保持皮面朝上，这样鸡皮不会变软，保持酥脆口感。
- Don't skip searing — the browned bits on the pan bottom dissolve into the sauce and give it deep, savory flavor.
- 不要省略煎鸡腿的步骤——锅底焦香物会融入酱汁，赋予其浓郁的鲜味层次。
- No olives? Simply omit them, or add a tablespoon of capers for a similar briny note.
- 没有橄榄？可以省略，或用 1 汤匙续随子（capers）代替，同样能带来咸鲜风味。
- Serve with steamed rice, crusty bread, or egg noodles to soak up the tomato sauce.
- 搭配米饭、法棍或鸡蛋面，充分吸收番茄酱汁。
- Leftovers keep in the fridge for 3 days and taste even better the next day.
- 剩余炖鸡冷藏可保存 3 天，隔夜后风味更佳。
