# Ratatouille / 普罗旺斯炖蔬菜

> **Cuisine:** French | **菜系：** 法式
> **Serves:** 4 | **份量：** 4 人份
> **Prep time:** 15 min | **准备时间：** 15 分钟
> **Cook time:** 25 min | **烹饪时间：** 25 分钟

## Instructions / 步骤

### English

1. Heat 2 tablespoons of olive oil in a large deep skillet or Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Add eggplant and cook for 4–5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until lightly browned. Transfer to a bowl.
2. Add the remaining 1 tablespoon of oil to the same pan. Add the onion and bell peppers and cook for 4 minutes until softened. Add garlic and cook for 30 seconds.
3. Add the zucchini, thyme, and oregano. Stir and cook for 2 minutes.
4. Return the eggplant to the pan. Pour in the crushed tomatoes and stir everything together. Season with salt and black pepper.
5. Reduce heat to medium-low, cover, and simmer for 12–15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until all vegetables are tender and the sauce has thickened.
6. Stir in the balsamic vinegar. Taste and adjust seasoning. Top with fresh basil if using and serve.

### 中文

1. 大火加热 2 汤匙橄榄油，放入茄子丁翻炒 4～5 分钟至略微上色，盛出备用。
2. 同锅加入剩余 1 汤匙橄榄油，放入洋葱和灯笼椒，炒 4 分钟至软化。加入蒜末翻炒 30 秒至出香。
3. 放入西葫芦、干百里香和干牛至，翻炒 2 分钟。
4. 将茄子倒回锅中，倒入番茄碎，全部搅拌均匀，用盐和黑胡椒调味。
5. 转中小火，盖上锅盖，偶尔翻拌，炖 12～15 分钟至所有蔬菜软烂、酱汁浓稠。
6. 加入意式黑醋搅匀，尝味调整咸淡。放上新鲜罗勒叶（可选）即可上桌。

## Notes / 备注

- Cooking the eggplant separately first prevents it from turning mushy and allows it to develop some color.
- 先单独炒茄子，可以避免它变得过烂，同时让茄子略微上色更香。
- Balsamic vinegar at the end brightens the whole dish and adds a sweet-tangy depth.
- 最后加意式黑醋能提亮整道菜的风味，带来甜酸层次感。
- Ratatouille tastes even better the next day as the flavors meld overnight.
- 普罗旺斯炖蔬菜隔夜后味道更佳，各种风味充分融合。
- Serve as a side dish, over steamed rice, with crusty bread, or tossed with pasta.
- 可作为配菜，也可搭配米饭、法棍或意面食用。
- Leftovers keep in the fridge for up to 4 days or can be frozen for 1 month.
- 剩余炖菜冷藏可保存 4 天，或冷冻保存 1 个月。
