# Borscht (Shanghai Style) / 罗宋汤

> **Cuisine:** Chinese (Shanghai) | **菜系：** 中式（上海）
> **Serves:** 4 | **份量：** 4 人份
> **Prep time:** 15 min | **准备时间：** 15 分钟
> **Cook time:** 45 min | **烹饪时间：** 45 分钟

## Ingredients / 食材

| Amount | Ingredient | 用量 | 食材 |
|--------|-----------|------|------|
| 300 g | beef shank or brisket, cut into 2 cm cubes | 300 克 | 牛腩或牛腱，切 2 厘米小块 |
| 2 medium | tomatoes, roughly chopped | 2 个中等 | 西红柿，切块 |
| 2 tbsp | tomato paste | 2 汤匙 | 番茄酱 |
| 2 medium | potatoes, peeled and diced | 2 个中等 | 土豆，去皮切块 |
| 2 | carrots, sliced | 2 根 | 胡萝卜，切片 |
| ¼ head | cabbage, roughly chopped | ¼ 颗 | 卷心菜，切大块 |
| 1 | onion, diced | 1 个 | 洋葱，切丁 |
| 2 cloves | garlic, minced | 2 瓣 | 大蒜，切末 |
| 1 tbsp | butter | 1 汤匙 | 黄油 |
| 1 tbsp | vegetable oil | 1 汤匙 | 植物油 |
| 800 ml | beef broth or water | 800 毫升 | 牛肉高汤或清水 |
| 1 tsp | sugar | 1 茶匙 | 白糖 |
| 1 tsp | salt (adjust to taste) | 1 茶匙 | 盐（按口味调整） |
| ½ tsp | black pepper | ½ 茶匙 | 黑胡椒粉 |
| 2 | bay leaves | 2 片 | 香叶 |
| 1 tbsp | Worcestershire sauce (optional) | 1 汤匙 | 伍斯特酱（可选） |

---

## Instructions / 步骤

### English

1. Blanch the beef: place beef cubes in cold water, bring to a boil, cook 2 minutes, then drain and rinse. Set aside.
2. In a large pot, heat butter and oil over medium heat. Add onion and garlic, sauté 2–3 minutes until soft.
3. Add tomatoes and tomato paste. Stir and cook 3–4 minutes until the tomatoes break down and the paste turns deep red.
4. Add the blanched beef and stir to coat with the tomato base. Pour in the broth, add bay leaves, and bring to a boil.
5. Reduce heat to medium-low, cover, and simmer for 20 minutes.
6. Add potatoes and carrots. Simmer uncovered for 10 minutes.
7. Add cabbage and cook another 5 minutes until the vegetables are tender.
8. Season with sugar, salt, black pepper, and Worcestershire sauce if using. Taste and adjust. Remove bay leaves and serve hot.

### 中文

1. 焯水处理牛肉：将牛肉块冷水下锅，煮开后再煮 2 分钟，捞出冲洗干净，备用。
2. 大锅中火放入黄油和植物油，待融化后加入洋葱和大蒜，翻炒 2–3 分钟至软透。
3. 加入西红柿块和番茄酱，翻炒 3–4 分钟至西红柿出汁、酱汁变成深红色。
4. 加入焯好的牛肉，翻炒均匀，倒入高汤，放入香叶，大火烧开。
5. 转中小火，盖锅盖炖煮 20 分钟。
6. 加入土豆和胡萝卜，开盖继续炖 10 分钟。
7. 加入卷心菜，再煮 5 分钟至蔬菜软烂。
8. 加入白糖、盐、黑胡椒调味，喜欢的话加入伍斯特酱，尝味后调整。取出香叶，趁热上桌。

---

## Notes / 备注

- For more tender beef in less time, cut pieces smaller (1 cm) or use a pressure cooker for 15 minutes at step 5.
- The sugar-tomato balance is key to the Shanghai style — start with 1 tsp and add more to taste.
- Leftovers thicken overnight and taste even better the next day; add a splash of water when reheating.
- 想缩短炖煮时间，可将牛肉切得更小（1 厘米），或在第 5 步改用压力锅压 15 分钟。
- 糖和番茄的平衡是上海口味的关键，先放 1 茶匙，再按口味增加。
- 隔夜汤汁会变浓，味道更醇厚；加热时加少量水稀释即可。
