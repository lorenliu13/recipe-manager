# Baby Bok Choy and Egg Soup / 小白菜鸡蛋汤

> **Cuisine:** Chinese | **菜系：** 中式
> **Serves:** 2 | **份量：** 2 人份
> **Prep time:** 5 min | **准备时间：** 5 分钟
> **Cook time:** 10 min | **烹饪时间：** 10 分钟

---

## Ingredients / 食材

| Amount | Ingredient | 用量 | 食材 |
|--------|-----------|------|------|
| 300 g | baby bok choy, cleaned and halved | 300 克 | 小白菜，洗净切半 |
| 2 | eggs, beaten | 2 个 | 鸡蛋，打散 |
| 3 cloves | garlic, minced | 3 瓣 | 大蒜，切末 |
| 600 ml | water or light chicken stock | 600 毫升 | 清水或淡鸡汤 |
| 1 tbsp | neutral oil | 1 汤匙 | 食用油 |
| 1 tsp | sesame oil | 1 茶匙 | 香油 |
| 1 tsp | soy sauce | 1 茶匙 | 生抽 |
| to taste | salt and white pepper | 适量 | 盐和白胡椒粉 |

---

## Instructions / 步骤

### English

1. Heat oil in a pot over medium-high heat. Add garlic and sauté for 30 seconds until fragrant.
2. Pour in water or stock and bring to a boil.
3. Add baby bok choy and cook for 2–3 minutes until tender but still bright green.
4. Add soy sauce and season with salt and white pepper.
5. Slowly drizzle in beaten egg while stirring the soup in a circular motion to create egg ribbons.
6. Remove from heat, drizzle with sesame oil, and serve immediately.

### 中文

1. 锅中倒油，中大火加热，下蒜末爆香约 30 秒。
2. 倒入清水或鸡汤，大火烧开。
3. 放入小白菜，煮 2–3 分钟至熟软但仍保持翠绿。
4. 加生抽，以盐和白胡椒粉调味。
5. 将打散的鸡蛋缓慢淋入锅中，边淋边顺一个方向搅动，形成蛋花。
6. 关火，淋上香油，立即上桌。

---

## Notes / 备注

- Stir the soup in one direction before adding egg for silkier egg ribbons. / 淋蛋液前顺一个方向搅动汤，蛋花更细腻。
- Add a few drops of rice vinegar at the end for a subtle tang. / 出锅前加几滴米醋，增加清爽酸味。
- Substitute: spinach, watercress, or regular bok choy all work well. / 可用菠菜、西洋菜或普通白菜替代小白菜。
- Best eaten right away; reheating makes the egg rubbery. / 现煮现吃，隔夜再热蛋花口感变老。
