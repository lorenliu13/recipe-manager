# Chinese Stir-Fry Cabbage / 手撕包菜

> **Cuisine:** Chinese | **菜系：** 中式
> **Serves:** 2–3 | **份量：** 2～3 人份
> **Prep time:** 10 min | **准备时间：** 10 分钟
> **Cook time:** 8 min | **烹饪时间：** 8 分钟

---

## Ingredients / 食材

| Amount | Ingredient | 用量 | 食材 |
|--------|-----------|------|------|
| ½ head (400 g) | green cabbage | 半颗（约 400 克） | 圆白菜（包菜） |
| 4 cloves | garlic, thinly sliced | 4 瓣 | 大蒜，切薄片 |
| 2 tbsp | vegetable oil | 2 汤匙 | 食用油 |
| 1½ tbsp | soy sauce | 1.5 汤匙 | 生抽 |
| 1 tbsp | oyster sauce | 1 汤匙 | 蚝油 |
| 1 tsp | sugar | 1 茶匙 | 糖 |
| 1 tsp | sesame oil | 1 茶匙 | 香油 |
| ¼ tsp | salt | ¼ 茶匙 | 盐 |
| 2 stalks | scallions, cut into 1-inch pieces | 2 根 | 葱，切段 |
| 2 (optional) | dried red chillies | 2 个（可选） | 干红辣椒 |

---

## Instructions / 步骤

### English

1. Tear the cabbage into rough 2-inch pieces by hand — do not use a knife. Hand-tearing creates jagged edges that absorb the sauce better. Rinse and spin or pat completely dry.
2. Mix soy sauce, oyster sauce, and sugar together in a small bowl. Set aside.
3. Heat a wok or large skillet over the highest heat until smoking. Add oil and swirl to coat.
4. Add garlic (and dried chillies if using) and stir-fry for 20 seconds until fragrant, being careful not to burn.
5. Add the cabbage all at once. Spread it out and leave untouched for 30 seconds to get a light char on the edges, then toss vigorously for 2–3 minutes until the leaves are tender but still have a slight crunch.
6. Pour the sauce over the cabbage and toss for another 30 seconds until evenly coated.
7. Add scallions and drizzle with sesame oil. Give a final toss and plate immediately.

### 中文

1. 用手将包菜撕成约 5 厘米大小的不规则块——不要用刀切。手撕的毛边更易吸附酱汁，口感更好。洗净后彻底沥干或擦干水分。
2. 将生抽、蚝油和糖混合于小碗中，搅匀备用。
3. 炒锅大火加热至冒烟，倒入食用油，转动锅身使油均匀铺开。
4. 放入蒜片（若用干辣椒也一并加入），翻炒约 20 秒至香味飘出，注意不要炒焦。
5. 一次性倒入所有包菜，铺开后不要翻动，让菜边稍微炙出焦香（约 30 秒），再大力翻炒 2～3 分钟，至菜叶变软但仍保留一定脆度。
6. 淋入调好的酱汁，翻炒约 30 秒至均匀挂汁。
7. 加入葱段，淋上香油，快速翻炒均匀后立即出锅装盘。

## Notes / 备注

- Hand-tearing (手撕) is the defining technique of this dish — it gives better texture and sauce absorption than knife-cut cabbage.
- 手撕是这道菜的核心技法——撕出的毛边比刀切的断面更能挂汁、口感更好。
- The wok must be screaming hot before the cabbage goes in; a lukewarm wok steams the cabbage and makes it soggy.
- 下菜前炒锅必须烧至极热，温度不够会让菜出水变软，失去脆感。
- Omit the dried chillies for a completely mild version — the dish is still full of flavour from the garlic and oyster sauce.
- 不放干辣椒即为无辣版本，蒜香和蚝油的鲜味已非常足够。
- A splash of Shaoxing rice wine (绍兴料酒) added with the sauce gives a deeper, more restaurant-style flavour.
- 调酱时加入少许绍兴料酒，风味更接近餐厅出品。
- Best eaten immediately — cabbage softens quickly once plated.
- 此菜出锅后尽快食用，久放包菜会继续软化，影响口感。
