# Lettuce with Oyster Sauce / 蚝油生菜

> **Cuisine:** Chinese (Cantonese) | **菜系：** 中式（粤式）
> **Serves:** 2 | **份量：** 2 人份
> **Prep time:** 5 min | **准备时间：** 5 分钟
> **Cook time:** 10 min | **烹饪时间：** 10 分钟

---
![](<../../figures/Pasted image 20260607215418.png>)
## Ingredients / 食材

| Amount | Ingredient | 用量 | 食材 |
|--------|-----------|------|------|
| 1 head (~400 g) | romaine or round head lettuce | 1 颗（约 400 克） | 罗马生菜或圆生菜 |
| 3 cloves | garlic, minced | 3 瓣 | 大蒜，切末 |
| 2 tbsp | oyster sauce | 2 汤匙 | 蚝油 |
| 1 tsp | light soy sauce | 1 茶匙 | 生抽 |
| 1 tsp | sugar | 1 茶匙 | 白糖 |
| 3 tbsp | water | 3 汤匙 | 清水 |
| ½ tsp | cornstarch | ½ 茶匙 | 生粉（淀粉） |
| 1 tsp | sesame oil | 1 茶匙 | 芝麻油 |
| 2 tbsp | vegetable oil, divided | 2 汤匙 | 植物油，分两次用 |
| ½ tsp | salt (for blanching water) | ½ 茶匙 | 盐（焯水用） |

---

## Instructions / 步骤

### English

1. Mix the sauce: in a small bowl, combine oyster sauce, light soy sauce, sugar, water, and cornstarch. Stir until smooth and set aside.
2. Bring a large pot of water to a boil. Add salt and 1 tbsp of oil. Blanch the lettuce leaves for 30–45 seconds until just wilted but still bright green. Drain well and arrange on a serving plate.
3. Heat the remaining 1 tbsp oil in a small pan over medium-high heat. Add garlic and stir-fry for 30 seconds until fragrant but not burnt.
4. Pour the sauce mixture into the pan. Stir constantly until it thickens and bubbles, about 1 minute. Remove from heat and drizzle in sesame oil.
5. Pour the sauce evenly over the lettuce and serve immediately.

### 中文

1. 调酱汁：将蚝油、生抽、白糖、清水和生粉在小碗中混合，搅拌均匀备用。
2. 烧一锅开水，加入盐和 1 汤匙植物油，放入生菜焯烫 30–45 秒至刚刚变软、颜色仍翠绿为止。捞出沥干，摆盘。
3. 另起小锅，中大火加热剩余 1 汤匙植物油，下蒜末爆香约 30 秒，注意不要炒焦。
4. 倒入调好的酱汁，边搅拌边加热至酱汁变稠冒泡，约 1 分钟。关火后淋入芝麻油。
5. 将酱汁均匀浇在生菜上，立即上桌。

---

## Notes / 备注

- Adding oil to the blanching water keeps the lettuce glossy and bright green.
- Drain the lettuce thoroughly before plating — excess water dilutes the sauce.
- The sauce can be made ahead and refrigerated for up to 3 days; reheat gently before using.
- 焯水时加油可以让生菜保持油亮翠绿的色泽。
- 生菜沥干再摆盘，否则盘底积水会冲淡酱汁。
- 酱汁可提前做好，冷藏保存 3 天，用时重新加热即可。
