# Stir-Fried Chicken Leg / 小炒鸡腿肉

> **Cuisine:** Chinese | **菜系：** 中式
> **Serves:** 2 | **份量：** 2 人份
> **Prep time:** 15 min | **准备时间：** 15 分钟
> **Cook time:** 15 min | **烹饪时间：** 15 分钟

## Ingredients / 食材

| Amount | Ingredient | 用量 | 食材 |
|--------|-----------|------|------|
| 2 | chicken legs, deboned and cut into bite-size pieces | 2 个 | 鸡腿，去骨切块 |
| 1 medium | onion, sliced | 1 个（中） | 洋葱，切丝 |
| 1 | green bell pepper, sliced | 1 个 | 青椒，切丝 |
| 1–2 | millet chili peppers, sliced (optional) | 1–2 个 | 小米辣，切片（可省略） |
| 3 cloves | garlic, minced | 3 瓣 | 大蒜，切末 |
| 2 tbsp | soy sauce | 2 汤匙 | 生抽 |
| 1 tbsp | oyster sauce | 1 汤匙 | 蚝油 |
| 1 tsp | dark soy sauce | 1 茶匙 | 老抽 |
| 1 tsp | sugar | 1 茶匙 | 白糖 |
| 1 tsp | cornstarch | 1 茶匙 | 生粉（淀粉） |
| 2 tbsp | neutral oil | 2 汤匙 | 食用油 |
| to taste | salt | 适量 | 盐 |

---

## Instructions / 步骤

### English

1. Debone chicken legs and cut into bite-size pieces. Marinate with 1 tbsp soy sauce, dark soy sauce, and cornstarch for 10 minutes.
2. Mix sauce: combine remaining 1 tbsp soy sauce, oyster sauce, and sugar in a small bowl. Set aside.
3. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a wok over high heat. Add chicken and sear undisturbed for 2 minutes, then stir-fry for another 2–3 minutes until golden and cooked through. Remove and set aside.
4. Add remaining 1 tbsp oil. Stir-fry garlic (and millet chili if using) for 30 seconds until fragrant.
5. Add onion and stir-fry for 2 minutes until softened and slightly caramelized.
6. Add green bell pepper and toss for 1 minute.
7. Return chicken to the pan. Pour in the sauce, toss everything to coat, and cook for 1 minute.
8. Taste and adjust salt. Serve immediately over steamed rice.

### 中文

1. 鸡腿去骨切块，加入 1 汤匙生抽、老抽和生粉，抓匀腌制 10 分钟。
2. 调碗汁：将剩余 1 汤匙生抽、蚝油、白糖混合均匀，备用。
3. 锅中倒 1 汤匙油，大火烧热。下鸡腿肉，煎 2 分钟不动，再翻炒 2–3 分钟至金黄熟透，盛出备用。
4. 锅中加剩余 1 汤匙油，下蒜末（及小米辣，若用）爆香约 30 秒。
5. 下洋葱翻炒约 2 分钟，炒至微软、边缘略焦糖化。
6. 下青椒翻炒 1 分钟。
7. 鸡腿肉回锅，倒入碗汁，翻炒均匀，再炒 1 分钟使入味。
8. 试味，按需加盐。立即搭配白米饭上桌。

## Notes / 备注

- Keeping the skin on adds flavor and a slightly crispy texture; remove it for a leaner dish. / 保留鸡皮更香且略带焦脆口感；去皮则更清淡。
- Omit millet chili entirely for a mild version — the dish is still full of flavor. / 不加小米辣也很鲜香，适合不吃辣的口味。
- Onion caramelizes quickly over high heat; don't skip this step — it adds sweetness that balances the savory sauce. / 大火快炒洋葱至微焦，甜味能平衡咸鲜的酱汁。
- Leftovers keep for 2 days in the fridge; reheat in a pan to restore texture. / 剩菜冷藏可保存 2 天，回锅翻炒口感更好。
