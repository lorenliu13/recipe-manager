# Cilantro Beef Stir-Fry / 香菜炒牛肉

> **Cuisine:** Chinese | **菜系：** 中式
> **Serves:** 2 | **份量：** 2 人份
> **Prep time:** 15 min | **准备时间：** 15 分钟
> **Cook time:** 10 min | **烹饪时间：** 10 分钟

---

## Ingredients / 食材

| Amount | Ingredient | 用量 | 食材 |
|--------|-----------|------|------|
| 300 g | beef sirloin or flank steak, thinly sliced | 300 克 | 牛里脊或牛腩，切薄片 |
| 1 large bunch | fresh cilantro (stems and leaves) | 1 大把 | 新鲜香菜（茎叶均用） |
| 4 cloves | garlic, minced | 4 瓣 | 大蒜，切末 |
| 1 tsp | ginger, minced | 1 茶匙 | 生姜，切末 |
| 2 tbsp | soy sauce | 2 汤匙 | 生抽 |
| 1 tbsp | oyster sauce | 1 汤匙 | 蚝油 |
| 1 tsp | sesame oil | 1 茶匙 | 香油 |
| 1 tsp | cornstarch | 1 茶匙 | 生粉（淀粉） |
| 1 tsp | sugar | 1 茶匙 | 白糖 |
| 2 tbsp | neutral oil | 2 汤匙 | 食用油 |
| to taste | salt | 适量 | 盐 |

---

## Instructions / 步骤

### English

1. Slice beef thinly against the grain. In a bowl, combine beef with soy sauce, cornstarch, and sesame oil. Mix well and marinate for 10 minutes.
2. Wash cilantro thoroughly. Cut stems into 2-inch pieces; keep leaves whole or roughly chopped.
3. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a wok or skillet over high heat. Add beef in a single layer and sear undisturbed for 60 seconds, then stir-fry for another 30 seconds until just cooked. Remove from pan and set aside.
4. Add remaining 1 tbsp oil to the pan. Stir-fry garlic and ginger for 30 seconds until fragrant.
5. Add cilantro stems and stir-fry for 1 minute. Add oyster sauce and sugar, toss to combine.
6. Return beef to the pan, add cilantro leaves, and toss everything together for 30 seconds.
7. Taste and adjust salt. Serve immediately over steamed rice.

### 中文

1. 牛肉逆纹切薄片。加入生抽、生粉和香油，抓匀后腌制 10 分钟。
2. 香菜洗净，茎切成约 5 厘米小段，叶子保留或略切。
3. 锅中倒 1 汤匙油，大火烧热。牛肉平铺入锅，煎约 60 秒不动，再翻炒 30 秒至刚熟，盛出备用。
4. 锅中加剩余 1 汤匙油，下蒜末和姜末爆香约 30 秒。
5. 放入香菜茎，翻炒 1 分钟，加蚝油和白糖，翻炒均匀。
6. 将牛肉回锅，放入香菜叶，快速翻炒 30 秒，让所有食材混合均匀。
7. 试味，按需加盐。立即搭配白米饭上桌。

---

## Notes / 备注

- Slice beef as thin as possible (partially freezing for 20 min makes this easier) for fast, tender results. / 牛肉切越薄越好（冷冻 20 分钟后更易切），炒出来更嫩。
- Use both stems and leaves — stems hold up to heat while leaves add fresh aroma at the end. / 茎叶都用：茎耐热先炒，叶子最后放以保留清香。
- Substitute: flank steak, sirloin, or even ground beef work well. / 可用牛腩、腰肉替代，或改用牛肉末。
- Best eaten right away; cilantro wilts quickly. / 现炒现吃，香菜久放易软塌。
