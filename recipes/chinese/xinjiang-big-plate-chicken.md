# Xinjiang Big Plate Chicken / 新疆大盘鸡

> **Cuisine:** Chinese — Xinjiang | **菜系：** 中式 — 新疆
> **Serves:** 4 | **份量：** 4 人份
> **Prep time:** 20 min | **准备时间：** 20 分钟
> **Cook time:** 45 min | **烹饪时间：** 45 分钟

## Instructions / 步骤

### English

1. Blanch the chicken pieces in boiling water for 3 minutes to remove impurities. Drain, rinse under cold water, and pat dry.
2. Heat oil in a wok or heavy pot over medium-high heat. Add Sichuan peppercorns, star anise, and bay leaf. Stir-fry for 30 seconds until fragrant.
3. Add ginger, garlic, scallion whites, and dried chillies. Stir-fry for 1 minute.
4. Add doubanjiang and stir-fry for 1 minute until the oil turns red.
5. Add the chicken pieces. Turn heat to high and stir-fry for 4–5 minutes until lightly browned on all sides.
6. Add Shaoxing wine, light soy sauce, dark soy sauce, and sugar. Toss to coat.
7. Pour in water or stock until the chicken is just covered. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to medium-low. Cover and simmer for 15 minutes.
8. Add potato chunks. Cover and cook for another 15 minutes until the potatoes are tender and the sauce has thickened.
9. Add the onion and bell peppers. Stir-fry uncovered on high heat for 3–4 minutes until the vegetables are just cooked and the sauce clings to everything. Taste and adjust salt.
10. Meanwhile, cook the noodles in a large pot of boiling salted water until just tender. Drain and pile onto a large serving plate.
11. Pour the big plate chicken and all its sauce directly over the noodles. Scatter scallion greens on top and serve family-style.

### 中文

1. 鸡块冷水下锅，焯水 3 分钟去除血沫，捞出用冷水冲洗干净，沥干备用。
2. 炒锅或厚底锅中火加热食用油，放入花椒、八角和香叶，翻炒约 30 秒至香味飘出。
3. 加入姜片、蒜瓣、葱白段和干红辣椒，翻炒约 1 分钟。
4. 加入郫县豆瓣酱，翻炒约 1 分钟至油色变红。
5. 放入鸡块，转大火翻炒 4～5 分钟，至鸡块表面微微上色。
6. 加入绍兴料酒、生抽、老抽和糖，翻炒均匀挂色。
7. 加入清水或鸡汤，水量刚好没过鸡块即可。大火烧开后转中小火，盖锅盖焖煮 15 分钟。
8. 放入土豆块，继续盖锅盖煮约 15 分钟，至土豆熟透、汤汁收浓。
9. 加入洋葱和青红椒块，转大火开盖翻炒 3～4 分钟，至蔬菜断生、汤汁均匀裹附。尝味，按需补盐。
10. 同时将宽面条下入沸腾的加盐清水中，煮至刚好熟透，捞出沥干，铺在大盘底部。
11. 将大盘鸡连同汤汁直接浇在面条上，撒上葱绿，上桌共享。

## Notes / 备注

- For a milder version, reduce dried chillies to 1–2 and use mild doubanjiang, or replace it with 1 tbsp tomato paste + ½ tsp paprika.
- 减辣版本：干辣椒减至 1～2 个，选用不辣的豆瓣酱，或用 1 汤匙番茄酱 + ½ 茶匙红椒粉代替豆瓣酱。
- Bone-in chicken is essential — it keeps the meat moist during the long braise and adds flavour to the sauce.
- 必须用带骨鸡块——骨头在长时间炖煮中既能保持肉质鲜嫩，又能为汤汁增添浓郁风味。
- If you can't find wide flat noodles, any thick noodle works: udon, hand-pulled noodles, or even fettuccine.
- 若买不到皮带面，可用乌冬面、手擀面，甚至意大利宽面条代替。
- The dish is intentionally saucy — the noodles soak up the braising liquid, which is considered the best part.
- 这道菜汤汁充足是关键——面条吸饱卤汁才是精髓所在。
- Leftovers reheat well; the flavours deepen overnight.
- 隔夜回锅风味更佳，汤汁更加醇厚。
