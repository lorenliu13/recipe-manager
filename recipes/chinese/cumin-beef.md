# Cumin Beef / 孜然牛肉

> **Cuisine:** Chinese | **菜系：** 中式
> **Serves:** 2 | **份量：** 2 人份
> **Prep time:** 15 min | **准备时间：** 15 分钟
> **Cook time:** 10 min | **烹饪时间：** 10 分钟

---

## Ingredients / 食材

| Amount | Ingredient | 用量 | 食材 |
|--------|-----------|------|------|
| 300 g | beef sirloin or flank steak, thinly sliced | 300 克 | 牛里脊或牛腩，切薄片 |
| 1 medium | onion, sliced | 1 个（中） | 洋葱，切丝 |
| 1 | green bell pepper, sliced | 1 个 | 青椒，切丝 |
| 2 tsp | whole cumin seeds | 2 茶匙 | 孜然粒 |
| 1 tsp | ground cumin | 1 茶匙 | 孜然粉 |
| 3 cloves | garlic, minced | 3 瓣 | 大蒜，切末 |
| 2 tbsp | soy sauce | 2 汤匙 | 生抽 |
| 1 tbsp | oyster sauce | 1 汤匙 | 蚝油 |
| 1 tsp | dark soy sauce | 1 茶匙 | 老抽 |
| 1 tsp | sugar | 1 茶匙 | 白糖 |
| 1 tsp | cornstarch | 1 茶匙 | 生粉（淀粉） |
| 2 tbsp | neutral oil | 2 汤匙 | 食用油 |
| to taste | salt | 适量 | 盐 |

---

## Instructions / 步骤

### English

1. Slice beef thinly against the grain. Marinate with 1 tbsp soy sauce, dark soy sauce, and cornstarch for 10 minutes.
2. Mix sauce: combine remaining 1 tbsp soy sauce, oyster sauce, and sugar. Set aside.
3. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a wok over high heat. Add beef in a single layer and sear for 60 seconds, then stir-fry for 1 minute until just cooked. Remove and set aside.
4. Add remaining 1 tbsp oil. Add cumin seeds and toast for 20 seconds until fragrant and popping.
5. Add garlic and stir-fry for 20 seconds. Add onion and cook for 2 minutes until softened.
6. Add green bell pepper and stir-fry for 1 minute.
7. Return beef to the pan. Add ground cumin and pour in the sauce. Toss everything over high heat for 1 minute.
8. Taste and adjust salt. Serve immediately over steamed rice or flatbread.

### 中文

1. 牛肉逆纹切薄片，加入 1 汤匙生抽、老抽和生粉，抓匀腌制 10 分钟。
2. 调碗汁：将剩余 1 汤匙生抽、蚝油、白糖混合均匀，备用。
3. 锅中倒 1 汤匙油，大火烧热。牛肉平铺入锅，煎约 60 秒，再翻炒 1 分钟至刚熟，盛出备用。
4. 加剩余 1 汤匙油，下孜然粒，小火爆香约 20 秒至香气溢出、孜然粒跳动。
5. 下蒜末翻炒 20 秒，再下洋葱炒约 2 分钟至微软。
6. 下青椒翻炒 1 分钟。
7. 牛肉回锅，撒入孜然粉，倒入碗汁，大火翻炒 1 分钟，使食材均匀裹上酱汁和孜然香气。
8. 试味，按需加盐。立即搭配白米饭或饼上桌。

## Notes / 备注

- Toasting whole cumin seeds first is key — it releases far more aroma than ground cumin alone. / 先爆孜然粒是关键，香气远比只用孜然粉浓郁。
- Partially freezing beef (20 min) makes thin slicing much easier. / 牛肉放冰箱冷冻 20 分钟后更易切薄片。
- This dish is naturally mild; for extra warmth, add ½ tsp smoked paprika. / 此菜本身口味温和；如需增加风味层次，可加 ½ 茶匙烟熏辣椒粉。
- Also excellent wrapped in warm flour tortillas or pita. / 用面饼或皮塔饼卷着吃同样美味。
