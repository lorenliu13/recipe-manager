# Tomato and Egg Stir-Fry / 西红柿炒鸡蛋

> **Cuisine:** Chinese | **菜系：** 中式
> **Serves:** 2 | **份量：** 2 人份
> **Prep time:** 5 min | **准备时间：** 5 分钟
> **Cook time:** 10 min | **烹饪时间：** 10 分钟

---
![](<../../Pasted image 20260607215514.png>)
## Ingredients / 食材

| Amount | Ingredient | 用量 | 食材 |
|--------|-----------|------|------|
| 3 | eggs | 3 个 | 鸡蛋 |
| 2 medium | tomatoes | 2 个中等大小 | 西红柿 |
| 2 tbsp | vegetable oil | 2 汤匙 | 食用油 |
| 1 tsp | salt | 1 茶匙 | 盐 |
| 1 tsp | sugar | 1 茶匙 | 糖 |
| 2 stalks | scallions (optional) | 2 根（可选） | 葱 |

---

## Instructions / 步骤

### English

1. Beat eggs with a pinch of salt until well combined.
2. Cut tomatoes into wedges.
3. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a wok over high heat. Pour in eggs and scramble until just set, then remove and set aside.
4. Add remaining oil to the wok. Stir-fry tomatoes for 2 minutes until they release their juices.
5. Return eggs to the wok. Season with salt and sugar. Toss together for 1 minute.
6. Garnish with scallions and serve with steamed rice.

### 中文

1. 鸡蛋打散，加少许盐搅拌均匀。
2. 西红柿切成块状。
3. 锅中加入1汤匙食用油，大火烧热后倒入蛋液，翻炒至凝固后盛出备用。
4. 再加入剩余食用油，放入西红柿翻炒约2分钟，至出汁。
5. 将鸡蛋倒回锅中，加盐和糖调味，翻炒均匀约1分钟。
6. 撒上葱花，搭配米饭即可上桌。

---

## Notes / 备注

- Adding a pinch of sugar balances the acidity of the tomatoes.
- 加少许糖可以中和西红柿的酸味，口感更好。
- For a saucier dish, add 2 tbsp water when stir-frying the tomatoes.
- 如果喜欢多一点汤汁，可以在炒西红柿时加入2汤匙清水。
