# Stir-Fried Cauliflower / 清炒花菜

> **Cuisine:** Chinese | **菜系：** 中式
> **Serves:** 2 | **份量：** 2 人份
> **Prep time:** 10 min | **准备时间：** 10 分钟
> **Cook time:** 10 min | **烹饪时间：** 10 分钟

---

## Ingredients / 食材

| Amount | Ingredient | 用量 | 食材 |
|--------|-----------|------|------|
| 1 head (~600 g) | cauliflower, broken into small florets | 1 颗（约 600 克） | 花菜，掰成小朵 |
| ½ | onion, thinly sliced | ½ 个 | 洋葱，切丝 |
| 1 tsp | fresh ginger, minced | 1 茶匙 | 姜末 |
| 3 cloves | garlic, thinly sliced | 3 瓣 | 大蒜，切片 |
| 1 tbsp | seafood soy sauce (or light soy sauce) | 1 汤匙 | 海鲜酱油（或生抽） |
| ½ tsp | zest of orange or pomelo peel, finely minced | ½ 茶匙 | 橘子皮或柚子皮末（切碎） |
| ½ tsp | salt (adjust to taste) | ½ 茶匙 | 盐（按口味调整） |
| 1½ tbsp | vegetable oil | 1½ 汤匙 | 植物油 |

---

## Instructions / 步骤

### English

1. Bring a large pot of water to a boil. Add cauliflower florets and blanch just until they turn slightly translucent, about 60–90 seconds. Do not overcook. Drain and set aside.
2. Heat oil in a wok or large skillet over medium-high heat. Add ginger, garlic, and onion. Stir-fry until the garlic slices are just lightly golden, about 1–2 minutes.
3. Add the blanched cauliflower. Stir-fry quickly over high heat for 2–3 minutes, tossing to coat everything evenly.
4. Drizzle in the seafood soy sauce and season with salt. Toss once more and transfer to a plate immediately.
5. Scatter the citrus zest on top and serve at once.

### 中文

1. 烧一锅开水，放入花菜焯烫约 60–90 秒，看到花菜变通透即可捞出，不要焯太久，保留脆感。沥干备用。
2. 锅中大火热油，放入姜末、蒜片和洋葱丝，翻炒 1–2 分钟至蒜片略微焦黄。
3. 下入焯好的花菜，大火翻炒 2–3 分钟，炒匀即可。
4. 淋入海鲜酱油，加盐调味，再翻炒均匀，立即出锅盛盘。
5. 将橘子皮或柚子皮末撒在上面，趁热上桌。

---

## Notes / 备注

- Blanching time is critical — pull the florets out the moment they turn translucent; over-blanching makes them mushy.
- The citrus zest is a small trick from a noodle shop: just a pinch lifts the whole dish with a clean, fresh fragrance.
- Seafood soy sauce is slightly sweeter and more aromatic than regular soy sauce; light soy sauce works fine as a substitute.
- 焯水时间是关键，花菜一变通透就要立刻捞出，焯过头口感会变软烂。
- 橘子皮或柚子皮末是点睛之笔，只需一点点，清香提味效果很好。
- 海鲜酱油比普通生抽略甜且香味更浓，用普通生抽代替也完全可以。
