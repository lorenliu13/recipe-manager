# Minced Pork with Eggplant / 肉末茄子

> **Cuisine:** Chinese | **菜系：** 中式
> **Serves:** 2 | **份量：** 2 人份
> **Prep time:** 10 min | **准备时间：** 10 分钟
> **Cook time:** 20 min | **烹饪时间：** 20 分钟

---

## Ingredients / 食材

| Amount | Ingredient | 用量 | 食材 |
|--------|-----------|------|------|
| 2 medium | eggplants (~500 g), cut into bite-size chunks | 2 个（中，约 500 克） | 茄子，切滚刀块 |
| 200 g | ground pork | 200 克 | 猪肉末 |
| 4 cloves | garlic, minced | 4 瓣 | 大蒜，切末 |
| 1 tsp | ginger, minced | 1 茶匙 | 生姜，切末 |
| 2 tbsp | soy sauce | 2 汤匙 | 生抽 |
| 1 tbsp | oyster sauce | 1 汤匙 | 蚝油 |
| 1 tsp | dark soy sauce | 1 茶匙 | 老抽 |
| 1 tsp | sugar | 1 茶匙 | 白糖 |
| 1 tsp | rice vinegar | 1 茶匙 | 米醋 |
| 3 tbsp | neutral oil | 3 汤匙 | 食用油 |
| 2 | spring onions, sliced (for garnish) | 2 根 | 葱花（装饰用） |
| to taste | salt | 适量 | 盐 |

---

## Instructions / 步骤

### English

1. Mix sauce: combine soy sauce, oyster sauce, dark soy sauce, sugar, and rice vinegar in a small bowl. Set aside.
2. Heat 2 tbsp oil in a wok or large skillet over high heat. Add eggplant and stir-fry for 6–8 minutes, tossing occasionally, until soft and slightly browned. Remove and set aside.
3. Add remaining 1 tbsp oil to the pan. Add ground pork and cook, breaking it up, for 3–4 minutes until browned.
4. Add garlic and ginger, stir-fry for 30 seconds until fragrant.
5. Return eggplant to the pan. Pour in the sauce and toss everything together over high heat for 2 minutes until well coated and glossy.
6. Taste and adjust salt. Garnish with spring onions and serve over steamed rice.

### 中文

1. 调碗汁：将生抽、蚝油、老抽、白糖和米醋混合均匀，备用。
2. 锅中倒 2 汤匙油，大火烧热。下茄子翻炒 6–8 分钟，偶尔翻动，至软熟略带焦色，盛出备用。
3. 锅中加剩余 1 汤匙油，下猪肉末，边炒边用铲子压散，炒 3–4 分钟至变色出香。
4. 下蒜末和姜末，翻炒约 30 秒至出香。
5. 茄子回锅，倒入碗汁，大火翻炒 2 分钟，使茄子充分裹上酱汁，色泽油亮。
6. 试味，按需加盐。撒上葱花，搭配白米饭上桌。

---

## Notes / 备注

- Salting eggplant before cooking is not necessary here — high-heat stir-frying in enough oil achieves the same result faster. / 茄子无需提前盐腌，大火足量油翻炒即可同样去苦出软。
- Chinese eggplants (long, purple) are ideal; they're less bitter and softer than globe eggplants. / 推荐用长条紫茄子，比圆茄子更少苦味、更易软熟。
- Rice vinegar adds a subtle tang that brightens the savory sauce — don't skip it. / 米醋提供轻微酸味，令咸鲜酱汁更清爽，不宜省略。
- Leftovers reheat well; the eggplant absorbs more flavor overnight. / 隔夜更入味，第二天回锅或微波加热均可。
