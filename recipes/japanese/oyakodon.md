# Oyakodon / 亲子丼

> **Cuisine:** Japanese | **菜系：** 日式
> **Serves:** 2 | **份量：** 2 人份
> **Prep time:** 10 min | **准备时间：** 10 分钟
> **Cook time:** 15 min | **烹饪时间：** 15 分钟

---

## Ingredients / 食材

| Amount | Ingredient | 用量 | 食材 |
|--------|-----------|------|------|
| 300 g | boneless chicken thigh, cut into bite-size pieces | 300 克 | 去骨鸡腿肉，切小块 |
| 3 large | eggs | 3 个（大） | 鸡蛋 |
| 1 medium | onion, thinly sliced | 1 个中等 | 洋葱，切薄片 |
| 1 cup | dashi stock (or low-sodium chicken broth) | 1 杯 | 日式高汤（或低钠鸡高汤） |
| 3 tbsp | soy sauce | 3 汤匙 | 生抽 |
| 2 tbsp | mirin | 2 汤匙 | 味醂 |
| 1 tbsp | sugar | 1 汤匙 | 糖 |
| 2 cups | steamed short-grain rice | 2 杯 | 蒸米饭（短粒米） |
| to garnish | sliced green onion | 适量 | 葱花 |

---

## Instructions / 步骤

### English

1. Combine dashi stock, soy sauce, mirin, and sugar in a small skillet or shallow pan. Stir and bring to a simmer over medium heat.
2. Add onion slices and cook 3 minutes until slightly softened.
3. Add chicken pieces in a single layer. Cover and cook over medium heat for 5–6 minutes until the chicken is just cooked through, flipping once halfway.
4. Lightly beat the eggs with chopsticks or a fork — leave some streaks of yolk and white for better texture. Do not over-beat.
5. Pour eggs in a slow, circular motion over the chicken. Cover and cook on low heat for 1–2 minutes until the egg is just barely set; the center should still be slightly runny.
6. Immediately slide the entire mixture over a bowl of steamed rice. Garnish with sliced green onion and serve at once.

### 中文

1. 将日式高汤、生抽、味醂和糖放入小平底锅或浅锅中，搅拌均匀，中火烧至微沸。
2. 加入洋葱片，煮3分钟至略微变软。
3. 将鸡肉块平铺入锅，加盖中火煮5–6分钟至鸡肉熟透，中途翻面一次。
4. 用筷子或叉子将鸡蛋轻轻打散，保留蛋黄和蛋白的条纹感，不要过度搅打。
5. 将蛋液以画圆的方式缓缓淋在鸡肉上，加盖小火煮1–2分钟，至蛋液刚刚凝固，中心仍略带流动感。
6. 立即将锅中所有食材滑盖在盛好米饭的碗上，撒上葱花，即刻食用。

---

## Notes / 备注

- Dashi stock (instant dashi powder) is available at Asian grocery stores or online; chicken broth is a reliable substitute.
- The signature texture is a half-set, custard-like egg — pull it off the heat while still slightly runny.
- Mirin is a sweet rice wine found in the Asian foods aisle; dry sherry + a pinch of sugar is a substitute.
- 即食柴鱼高汤粉在亚洲超市或网上可购；鸡高汤是可靠的替代品。
- 半凝固的溏心蛋是亲子丼的精髓，蛋液中心仍流动时立刻离火。
- 味醂在超市亚洲食品区有售；可用干雪莉酒加少许糖代替。
