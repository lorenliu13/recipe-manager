# Irish Beef Stew / 爱尔兰炖牛肉

> **Cuisine:** Irish | **菜系：** 爱尔兰
> **Serves:** 8 | **份量：** 8 人份
> **Prep time:** 30 min | **准备时间：** 30 分钟
> **Cook time:** 6 hrs (slow cooker High) | **烹饪时间：** 6 小时（慢炖锅高温档）

## Ingredients / 食材

| Amount | Ingredient | 用量 | 食材 |
|--------|-----------|------|------|
| 2 tbsp | olive oil | 2 汤匙 | 橄榄油 |
| 3 tbsp | all-purpose flour | 3 汤匙 | 中筋面粉 |
| 2 lbs | beef chuck, cut into 1½-inch cubes | 900 克 | 牛肩肉，切成 4 厘米方块 |
| 1 lb | carrots, peeled and cut into 1-inch chunks | 450 克 | 胡萝卜，削皮切块 |
| 6 large | potatoes, peeled and cut into large chunks | 6 个大 | 土豆，削皮切大块 |
| 1 | white onion, cut into large chunks | 1 个 | 白洋葱，切大块 |
| 2 cloves | garlic, minced | 2 瓣 | 大蒜，切末 |
| 2 cups | beef broth | 480 毫升 | 牛肉汤 |
| 1 (6 oz) can | tomato paste | 1 罐（170 克） | 番茄酱 |
| 1 (12 oz) can | Irish stout beer (e.g. Guinness) | 1 罐（355 毫升） | 爱尔兰黑啤酒（如健力士） |
| 1 tbsp | cold water | 1 汤匙 | 冷水 |
| 1 tbsp | cornstarch | 1 汤匙 | 玉米淀粉 |

---

## Instructions / 步骤

### English

1. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Toss beef cubes with flour until evenly coated, then fry in the hot oil until browned on all sides.
2. Place carrots, potatoes, onion, and garlic into a large slow cooker. Place the browned beef on top of the vegetables.
3. Stir together beef broth and tomato paste until combined, then pour into the slow cooker along with the beer.
4. Cover and cook on High for 6 hours or Low for 8 hours.
5. During the last hour, dissolve cornstarch in cold water and stir into the broth to thicken. Season with salt and pepper to taste and serve.

### 中文

1. 平底锅中火加热橄榄油。将牛肩肉块裹上面粉，放入热油中煎至四面上色。
2. 将胡萝卜、土豆、洋葱和大蒜放入大号慢炖锅中，再将煎好的牛肉放在蔬菜上面。
3. 将牛肉汤与番茄酱搅拌均匀，连同黑啤酒一起倒入慢炖锅。
4. 盖上盖子，高温档炖 6 小时，或低温档炖 8 小时。
5. 结束前 1 小时，将玉米淀粉溶于冷水后搅入汤中使其浓稠。按口味加盐和胡椒调味即可上桌。

---

## Notes / 备注

- Browning the beef before slow-cooking adds depth of flavor — don't skip this step.
- 煎牛肉这一步能增加层次感，请勿省略。
- Guinness is traditional, but any Irish stout works; for alcohol-free, substitute extra beef broth.
- 传统用健力士黑啤，也可换其他爱尔兰黑啤；若不用酒精，可用等量牛肉汤代替。
- Leftovers keep refrigerated for up to 3 days and taste even better the next day.
- 剩菜冷藏可保存 3 天，隔夜后味道更浓郁。
- **Note:** Cook time is 6–8 hours (slow cooker), well beyond the usual 30–45 min target — best for a weekend or when you can set it and forget it.
- **注意：** 本食谱需慢炖 6–8 小时，远超平日 30–45 分钟目标，适合周末或可长时间守候时制作。
