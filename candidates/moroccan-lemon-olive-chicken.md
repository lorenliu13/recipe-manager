# Moroccan Lemon Olive Chicken / 摩洛哥柠檬橄榄鸡

> **Cuisine:** Moroccan | **菜系：** 摩洛哥
> **Serves:** 2–3 | **份量：** 2–3 人份
> **Prep time:** 10 min | **准备时间：** 10 分钟
> **Cook time:** 35 min | **烹饪时间：** 35 分钟

---

## Ingredients / 食材

| Amount | Ingredient | 用量 | 食材 |
|--------|-----------|------|------|
| 500 g | bone-in chicken thighs | 500 克 | 带骨鸡腿 |
| 1/2 cup | green olives, pitted | 1/2 杯 | 绿橄榄，去核 |
| 1 | lemon, zested and juiced | 1 个 | 柠檬，取皮屑和汁 |
| 1 medium | onion, thinly sliced | 1 个中等 | 洋葱，切薄片 |
| 4 cloves | garlic, minced | 4 瓣 | 大蒜，切末 |
| 1 tsp | ground cumin | 1 茶匙 | 孜然粉 |
| 1/2 tsp | turmeric | 1/2 茶匙 | 姜黄粉 |
| 1/2 tsp | ground coriander | 1/2 茶匙 | 芫荽粉 |
| 1/2 tsp | paprika | 1/2 茶匙 | 红椒粉 |
| 2 tbsp | olive oil | 2 汤匙 | 橄榄油 |
| 1/2 cup | chicken broth | 1/2 杯 | 鸡高汤 |
| to taste | salt and black pepper | 适量 | 盐和黑胡椒 |
| to garnish | fresh cilantro or parsley | 适量 | 新鲜香菜或欧芹 |

---

## Instructions / 步骤

### English

1. Mix cumin, turmeric, coriander, paprika, salt, and pepper together. Rub the spice mixture all over the chicken pieces.
2. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Brown chicken skin-side down for 4 minutes, then flip and brown the other side for 2 minutes. Remove and set aside.
3. Add onion to the skillet and cook over medium heat until softened, about 4 minutes. Add garlic and cook 1 minute.
4. Pour in chicken broth, lemon zest, and lemon juice. Stir to combine and bring to a simmer.
5. Return chicken to the pan, skin-side up. Scatter olives around the chicken. Cover and simmer over medium-low heat for 20 minutes until the chicken is cooked through.
6. Uncover and simmer 5 minutes to reduce the sauce slightly. Garnish with cilantro or parsley and serve with bread or couscous.

### 中文

1. 将孜然粉、姜黄粉、芫荽粉、红椒粉、盐和黑胡椒混合，均匀涂抹在鸡腿上。
2. 大平底锅中大火加热橄榄油，鸡皮朝下煎4分钟，翻面再煎2分钟至表面金黄，取出备用。
3. 锅中放入洋葱，中火翻炒至变软，约4分钟。加入大蒜炒1分钟。
4. 倒入鸡高汤、柠檬皮屑和柠檬汁，搅拌均匀，烧至微沸。
5. 将鸡腿皮朝上放回锅中，周围撒上橄榄，加盖，中小火焖煮20分钟至鸡肉熟透。
6. 揭盖后继续煮5分钟收汁。撒上香菜或欧芹，搭配面包或古斯米食用。

---

## Notes / 备注

- Boneless chicken thighs also work; reduce the covered simmer time to 14 minutes.
- Kalamata olives can substitute for green olives for a deeper, more briny flavor.
- Serve with couscous, crusty bread, or steamed rice to soak up the sauce.
- 也可使用去骨鸡腿，将加盖焖煮时间缩短至14分钟。
- 可用卡拉马塔黑橄榄代替绿橄榄，风味更浓郁。
- 搭配古斯米、面包或米饭，用于蘸汁食用风味绝佳。
