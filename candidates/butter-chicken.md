# Butter Chicken / 黄油鸡

> **Cuisine:** Indian | **菜系：** 印度
> **Serves:** 2–3 | **份量：** 2–3 人份
> **Prep time:** 10 min | **准备时间：** 10 分钟
> **Cook time:** 35 min | **烹饪时间：** 35 分钟

---

## Ingredients / 食材

| Amount | Ingredient | 用量 | 食材 |
|--------|-----------|------|------|
| 500 g | boneless chicken thighs, cut into chunks | 500 克 | 去骨鸡腿肉，切块 |
| 1 can (400 g) | crushed tomatoes | 1 罐（400克） | 番茄碎罐头 |
| 3 tbsp | butter | 3 汤匙 | 黄油 |
| 1/2 cup | heavy cream | 1/2 杯 | 淡奶油 |
| 1 medium | onion, finely chopped | 1 个中等 | 洋葱，切碎 |
| 4 cloves | garlic, minced | 4 瓣 | 大蒜，切末 |
| 1 tsp | fresh ginger, grated | 1 茶匙 | 生姜，磨泥 |
| 1 tsp | garam masala | 1 茶匙 | 印度综合香料（葛拉姆马萨拉） |
| 1 tsp | ground cumin | 1 茶匙 | 孜然粉 |
| 1/2 tsp | ground coriander | 1/2 茶匙 | 芫荽粉 |
| 1/2 tsp | turmeric | 1/2 茶匙 | 姜黄粉 |
| 1/2 tsp | salt | 1/2 茶匙 | 盐 |
| to serve | steamed rice | 搭配 | 米饭 |

---

## Instructions / 步骤

### English

1. Melt 2 tbsp butter in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add chicken pieces in a single layer and cook until lightly browned on both sides, about 6 minutes total. Remove and set aside.
2. Add remaining 1 tbsp butter to the skillet. Cook onion over medium heat until soft and translucent, about 5 minutes. Add garlic and ginger and cook 1 minute until fragrant.
3. Stir in garam masala, cumin, coriander, and turmeric. Cook 30 seconds, stirring constantly.
4. Add crushed tomatoes, stir well to combine, and simmer over medium heat for 8 minutes until the sauce thickens slightly.
5. Return chicken to the pan, stir to coat in the sauce, and simmer 8 minutes until cooked through.
6. Stir in heavy cream, season with salt, and heat through for 2 minutes. Serve over steamed rice.

### 中文

1. 大平底锅中火融化2汤匙黄油，鸡肉块平铺入锅，两面各煎约3分钟至微黄，取出备用。
2. 加入剩余1汤匙黄油，中火翻炒洋葱至软透明，约5分钟。加入大蒜和生姜翻炒1分钟至香气溢出。
3. 加入葛拉姆马萨拉、孜然粉、芫荽粉和姜黄粉，不断翻炒30秒。
4. 倒入番茄碎，搅拌均匀，中火慢煮8分钟至酱汁略稠。
5. 将鸡肉放回锅中，裹匀酱汁，继续煮8分钟至鸡肉熟透。
6. 加入淡奶油，用盐调味，再加热2分钟。搭配米饭食用。

## Notes / 备注

- Garam masala is available in the spice aisle of most supermarkets; it is a mild pre-blended spice mix.
- For a richer sauce, blend the tomato-onion base with a hand blender before adding the cream.
- Leftovers keep refrigerated for 3 days and taste even better the next day.
- 葛拉姆马萨拉在大多数超市香料区均有售，口味温和。
- 想要更顺滑的酱汁，可在加奶油前用手持搅拌棒将番茄洋葱酱打匀。
- 剩余菜肴冷藏可保存3天，隔夜后味道更佳。
