# Mushroom Risotto / 意式蘑菇烩饭

> **Cuisine:** Italian | **菜系：** 意式
> **Serves:** 2 | **份量：** 2 人份
> **Prep time:** 10 min | **准备时间：** 10 分钟
> **Cook time:** 35 min | **烹饪时间：** 35 分钟

---

## Ingredients / 食材

| Amount | Ingredient | 用量 | 食材 |
|--------|-----------|------|------|
| 180 g | Arborio rice | 180 克 | 意式烩饭米（Arborio） |
| 250 g | mushrooms (cremini or button), sliced | 250 克 | 蘑菇（褐菇或口蘑），切片 |
| 1 medium | onion, finely diced | 1 个（中） | 洋葱，切细丁 |
| 3 cloves | garlic, minced | 3 瓣 | 大蒜，切末 |
| 800 ml | chicken or vegetable broth, warm | 800 毫升 | 鸡汤或蔬菜高汤，保温备用 |
| 3 tbsp | butter, divided | 3 汤匙 | 黄油，分次使用 |
| 1 tbsp | olive oil | 1 汤匙 | 橄榄油 |
| 40 g | Parmesan cheese, finely grated | 40 克 | 帕马森芝士，细磨碎 |
| 1 tsp | soy sauce | 1 茶匙 | 生抽 |
| ½ tsp | salt | ½ 茶匙 | 盐 |
| ¼ tsp | black pepper | ¼ 茶匙 | 黑胡椒 |
| 2 tbsp | fresh parsley, chopped | 2 汤匙 | 新鲜欧芹，切碎 |

---

## Instructions / 步骤

### English

1. Heat broth in a small saucepan over low heat; keep warm throughout cooking.
2. In a wide heavy pan, heat olive oil and 1 tbsp butter over medium-high heat. Add mushrooms in a single layer and cook undisturbed for 3 minutes, then stir and cook 2 more minutes until golden. Season with salt, stir in soy sauce, and transfer mushrooms to a plate.
3. Reduce heat to medium. In the same pan, melt 1 tbsp butter. Add onion and cook for 3–4 minutes until softened. Add garlic and cook 1 minute.
4. Add Arborio rice and stir for 1–2 minutes until the grains look translucent at the edges.
5. Add warm broth one ladleful (~120 ml) at a time, stirring frequently. Wait until each addition is almost fully absorbed before adding the next. Continue for 22–25 minutes until the rice is creamy and just tender with a slight bite.
6. Remove from heat. Stir in the remaining 1 tbsp butter, Parmesan, and black pepper. Fold the mushrooms back in.
7. Taste and adjust salt. Serve immediately, topped with parsley.

### 中文

1. 将高汤倒入小锅，小火保温，全程备用。
2. 宽口厚底锅中大火热橄榄油和 1 汤匙黄油，将蘑菇片平铺入锅，静置煎 3 分钟不翻动，再翻炒 2 分钟至金黄。加盐调味，淋入生抽翻炒均匀，盛出备用。
3. 转中火，锅中融化 1 汤匙黄油，加入洋葱丁翻炒 3–4 分钟至软化，加入蒜末再炒 1 分钟。
4. 加入 Arborio 米，翻炒 1–2 分钟至米粒边缘呈透明状。
5. 每次加入约 120 毫升温热高汤，频繁搅拌，等高汤几乎完全吸收后再加下一勺。如此重复约 22–25 分钟，至米饭呈乳脂状且带有轻微嚼劲。
6. 离火，拌入剩余 1 汤匙黄油、帕马森芝士碎和黑胡椒，再将煎好的蘑菇翻拌进去。
7. 尝味调整咸淡，立即盛盘，撒上欧芹碎即可。

---

## Notes / 备注

- Keep the broth warm throughout — cold broth shocks the rice and makes it cook unevenly.
- 全程保持高汤温热——冷高汤会让米饭受热不均，影响口感。
- Soy sauce is a small umami boost that replaces the need for extra salt and deepens the mushroom flavor.
- 少量生抽可提升鲜味，减少额外加盐，并加深蘑菇的香味层次。
- Arborio rice is widely stocked in supermarkets; do not rinse it before cooking — the surface starch is what makes risotto creamy.
- Arborio 米在大多数超市均有售；使用前不要淘洗，表面的淀粉正是烩饭乳脂感的来源。
- Risotto does not reheat well; best enjoyed fresh. Leftovers can be shaped into patties and pan-fried for arancini.
- 烩饭不易复热，建议现做现吃。剩余烩饭可捏成饼状，煎制成意式炸饭团（arancini）。
