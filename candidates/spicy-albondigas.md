# Spicy Albondigas Soup / 辣味肉丸汤

> **Cuisine:** Mexican | **菜系：** 墨西哥式
> **Serves:** 10 | **份量：** 10 人份
> **Prep time:** 30 min | **准备时间：** 30 分钟
> **Cook time:** 85 min | **烹饪时间：** 85 分钟

---

## Ingredients / 食材

### Meatballs / 肉丸

| Amount | Ingredient | 用量 | 食材 |
|--------|-----------|------|------|
| 2 lbs | ground beef | 900 克 | 牛肉馅 |
| ½ cup | uncooked white rice | ½ 杯 | 生白米 |
| ¼ cup | bread crumbs | ¼ 杯 | 面包糠 |
| 1 large | egg | 1 个大 | 鸡蛋 |
| 2 tsp | ground cumin | 2 茶匙 | 孜然粉 |
| ½ tsp | garlic powder | ½ 茶匙 | 蒜粉 |
| ¼ tsp | salt | ¼ 茶匙 | 盐 |

### Soup / 汤底

| Amount | Ingredient | 用量 | 食材 |
|--------|-----------|------|------|
| 10 cups | chicken broth | 10 杯 | 鸡汤 |
| 1 can (10 oz) | diced tomatoes with green chile peppers | 1 罐（280 克） | 青辣椒番茄丁罐头 |
| 1 can (8 oz) | tomato sauce | 1 罐（225 克） | 番茄酱罐头 |
| 1 can (7 oz) | diced green chiles, drained | 1 罐（200 克） | 青辣椒丁罐头，沥水 |
| 4 medium | carrots, sliced | 4 根中等 | 胡萝卜，切片 |
| 4 stalks | celery, sliced | 4 根 | 西芹，切片 |
| ½ large | onion, chopped | ½ 个大 | 洋葱，切碎 |
| 1 bunch | cilantro leaves, chopped | 1 把 | 香菜叶，切碎 |
| 2 tsp | ground cumin | 2 茶匙 | 孜然粉 |
| 1 tsp | dried oregano | 1 茶匙 | 干牛至 |
| ½ tsp | garlic powder | ½ 茶匙 | 蒜粉 |

---

## Instructions / 步骤

### English

1. Make the meatballs: Mix ground beef, uncooked rice, bread crumbs, egg, cumin, garlic powder, and salt in a large bowl until well combined. Form 1½-tablespoon portions into 1-inch meatballs.
2. Bring chicken broth to a boil in a large stockpot over medium heat.
3. Reduce heat to a simmer. Drop in meatballs and simmer until cooked through, about 20 minutes.
4. Add diced tomatoes, tomato sauce, and diced chiles. Stir in carrots, celery, onion, cilantro, cumin, oregano, and garlic powder.
5. Simmer until vegetables are tender and flavors are blended, about 1 hour.
6. Serve with warm flour tortillas.

### 中文

1. 制作肉丸：将牛肉馅、生白米、面包糠、鸡蛋、孜然粉、蒜粉和盐放入大碗中混合均匀。每份约 1½ 汤匙，搓成直径约 2.5 厘米的肉丸。
2. 大锅中倒入鸡汤，中火烧开。
3. 转小火，放入肉丸，炖约 20 分钟至熟透。
4. 加入番茄辣椒丁罐头、番茄酱和青辣椒丁，再放入胡萝卜、西芹、洋葱、香菜、孜然粉、牛至和蒜粉，搅拌均匀。
5. 小火慢炖约 1 小时，至蔬菜软烂、汤味浓郁。
6. 搭配热面饼一同上桌。

---

## Notes / 备注

- For a milder version, replace the diced tomatoes with green chiles with plain diced tomatoes, and use mild green chiles.
- 如需降低辣度，可将青辣椒番茄丁罐头换成普通番茄丁罐头，并选用不辣的青辣椒。
- Cook time is 85 minutes — plan ahead; the broth is worth it.
- 烹饪时间约 85 分钟，需提前安排，但汤底浓郁鲜美非常值得。
- Leftovers keep well in the refrigerator for up to 4 days; rice in the meatballs absorbs broth as it sits.
- 剩菜可冷藏保存 4 天；肉丸中的米粒会吸收汤汁，口感更软糯。
