# Tortilla Española / 西班牙土豆烘蛋

> **Cuisine:** Spanish | **菜系：** 西班牙
> **Serves:** 2–3 | **份量：** 2–3 人份
> **Prep time:** 10 min | **准备时间：** 10 分钟
> **Cook time:** 25 min | **烹饪时间：** 25 分钟

---

## Ingredients / 食材

| Amount | Ingredient | 用量 | 食材 |
|--------|-----------|------|------|
| 4 large | eggs | 4 个（大） | 鸡蛋 |
| 2 medium (~400 g) | potatoes, peeled and thinly sliced | 2 个中等（约400克） | 土豆，去皮切薄片 |
| 1 medium | onion, thinly sliced | 1 个中等 | 洋葱，切薄片 |
| 1/3 cup | olive oil | 1/3 杯 | 橄榄油 |
| 1/2 tsp | salt | 1/2 茶匙 | 盐 |
| to taste | black pepper | 适量 | 黑胡椒 |

---

## Instructions / 步骤

### English

1. Heat olive oil in a 20 cm non-stick skillet over medium heat. Add potatoes and onion, season with ¼ tsp salt. Cook, stirring occasionally, until tender and lightly golden, about 15 minutes.
2. Transfer potatoes and onion to a bowl with a slotted spoon, leaving about 1 tbsp oil in the pan.
3. Beat eggs with remaining ¼ tsp salt and pepper. Add the potato-onion mixture and stir gently to combine. Let sit 5 minutes.
4. Reheat the reserved oil in the skillet over medium heat. Pour in the egg mixture and spread evenly. Cook until the edges are set and the bottom is golden, about 5 minutes.
5. Place a large flat plate over the skillet. In one confident motion, flip the tortilla onto the plate. Slide it back into the skillet raw-side down. Cook 3 minutes until just set in the center.
6. Slide onto a plate. Serve warm or at room temperature, cut into wedges.

### 中文

1. 中火在20厘米不粘锅中加热橄榄油。放入土豆和洋葱，撒入1/4茶匙盐，偶尔翻炒，煮至软烂微黄，约15分钟。
2. 用漏勺将土豆和洋葱盛出，锅中保留约1汤匙油。
3. 将鸡蛋打散，加入剩余1/4茶匙盐和黑胡椒，放入土豆洋葱混合均匀，静置5分钟。
4. 中火加热锅中留存的油，倒入蛋液混合物铺平。煮至边缘凝固、底部金黄，约5分钟。
5. 将大平盘盖在锅上，果断将烘蛋翻扣到盘中，再将生面朝下滑回锅内，续煎3分钟至中心刚好凝固。
6. 滑入盘中，切块，温热或室温食用均可。

## Notes / 备注

- Waxy potatoes (e.g. Yukon Gold) hold their shape better than russets.
- The center should be slightly soft — Spaniards prefer a cuajada (just-set) texture, not fully cooked through.
- Leftovers keep in the fridge for up to 2 days and taste great cold.
- 蜡质土豆（如育空黄金）比粉质土豆更不易散开。
- 中心保持略软是西班牙传统口感，不要煎得全熟。
- 剩余烘蛋冷藏可保存2天，冷食同样美味。
