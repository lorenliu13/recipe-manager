# Tomato Basil Pasta / 番茄罗勒意面

> **Cuisine:** Italian | **菜系：** 意式
> **Serves:** 6 | **份量：** 6 人份
> **Prep time:** 10 min | **准备时间：** 10 分钟
> **Cook time:** 15 min | **烹饪时间：** 15 分钟

---
![](<../figures/Pasted image 20260607223801.png>)
## Ingredients / 食材

| Amount | Ingredient | 用量 | 食材 |
|--------|-----------|------|------|
| 2 cups | diced tomatoes | 2 杯 | 番茄丁 |
| 1 small | onion, finely chopped | 1 个小 | 洋葱，切碎 |
| 6 tbsp | olive oil | 6 汤匙 | 橄榄油 |
| 6 leaves | fresh basil, torn | 6 片 | 新鲜罗勒叶，撕碎 |
| 2 cloves | garlic, crushed | 2 瓣 | 大蒜，压碎 |
| 10 oz | fusilli pasta | 280 克 | 螺旋意面 |
| 1½ cups | crumbled feta cheese | 1½ 杯 | 菲达奶酪碎 |
| 3 tbsp | grated Parmesan cheese | 3 汤匙 | 帕玛森奶酪，磨碎 |
| to taste | salt and black pepper | 适量 | 盐和黑胡椒 |

---

## Instructions / 步骤

### English

1. Stir together tomatoes, onion, olive oil, basil, and garlic in a medium bowl; set aside.
2. Bring a large pot of lightly salted water to a boil. Cook fusilli until tender yet firm to the bite, about 12 minutes. Drain.
3. Toss warm pasta with feta and Parmesan in a large serving bowl.
4. Stir in tomato mixture until well combined. Season with salt and pepper.
5. Serve warm or at room temperature.

### 中文

1. 将番茄丁、洋葱碎、橄榄油、罗勒叶和大蒜放入中碗中搅拌均匀，备用。
2. 大锅中加水烧开，加少许盐，煮螺旋意面约 12 分钟至弹牙。捞出沥水。
3. 将热意面放入大碗中，加入菲达奶酪碎和帕玛森奶酪翻拌均匀。
4. 拌入番茄混合物，搅拌均匀。用盐和黑胡椒调味。
5. 趁热或冷却至室温后即可食用。

---

## Notes / 备注

- Can be served hot or cold — makes a great next-day lunch as a pasta salad.
- 可热食也可冷食，隔天作为冷意面沙拉同样美味。
- Substitute fusilli with penne or rotini if unavailable.
- 螺旋意面可用笔管面或车轮面代替。
- Store in the refrigerator for up to 3 days; stir before serving.
- 可冷藏保存 3 天，食用前搅拌一下即可。
