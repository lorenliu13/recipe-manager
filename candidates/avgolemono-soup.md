# Avgolemono Soup / 希腊柠檬鸡汤

> **Cuisine:** Greek | **菜系：** 希腊
> **Serves:** 2–3 | **份量：** 2–3 人份
> **Prep time:** 5 min | **准备时间：** 5 分钟
> **Cook time:** 30 min | **烹饪时间：** 30 分钟

---

## Ingredients / 食材

| Amount | Ingredient | 用量 | 食材 |
|--------|-----------|------|------|
| 1 L (4 cups) | chicken broth | 1升（4杯） | 鸡高汤 |
| 1.5 cups | cooked shredded chicken | 1.5 杯 | 熟鸡肉，手撕 |
| 1/3 cup | long-grain white rice | 1/3 杯 | 长粒白米 |
| 2 large | eggs | 2 个（大） | 鸡蛋 |
| 3 tbsp | fresh lemon juice (about 1 lemon) | 3 汤匙（约1个柠檬） | 新鲜柠檬汁 |
| to taste | salt and white pepper | 适量 | 盐和白胡椒 |
| optional | fresh parsley, chopped | 可选 | 新鲜欧芹，切碎 |

---

## Instructions / 步骤

### English

1. Bring chicken broth to a boil in a medium saucepan. Add rice, reduce heat to medium, and cook 15 minutes until the rice is tender.
2. Add shredded chicken and reduce heat to low.
3. In a medium bowl, whisk eggs until frothy, about 1 minute. Slowly whisk in lemon juice.
4. Ladle about 1 cup of hot broth into the egg-lemon mixture, whisking constantly to temper the eggs.
5. Pour the tempered mixture back into the pot while stirring gently. Cook over low heat for 2–3 minutes — do not let it boil or the eggs will curdle.
6. Season with salt and white pepper. Ladle into bowls, garnish with parsley, and serve immediately.

### 中文

1. 中型锅中将鸡高汤煮沸，加入白米，转中火煮15分钟至米粒软烂。
2. 放入手撕鸡肉，转小火。
3. 将鸡蛋在碗中打发至起泡，约1分钟，缓缓加入柠檬汁搅匀。
4. 从锅中舀约1杯热汤，边搅拌边缓缓倒入蛋液中进行升温，防止蛋液结块。
5. 将升温后的蛋液倒回锅中，轻轻搅拌，小火加热2–3分钟，切勿沸腾，否则鸡蛋会凝结。
6. 加盐和白胡椒调味，盛碗，撒欧芹，立即食用。

---

## Notes / 备注

- Tempering the eggs (step 4) is the key technique — add the hot liquid slowly while whisking continuously.
- Rotisserie chicken works great here to save time.
- Do not reheat leftovers to a boil; warm gently on low heat to keep the silky texture.
- 升温步骤（第4步）是关键，务必一边搅拌一边缓缓加入热汤。
- 使用超市熟食烤鸡可节省时间。
- 剩余汤品加热时勿沸腾，小火慢热以保持丝滑口感。
