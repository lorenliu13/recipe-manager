# French Onion Soup / 法式洋葱汤

> **Cuisine:** French | **菜系：** 法式
> **Serves:** 4 | **份量：** 4 人份
> **Prep time:** 10 min | **准备时间：** 10 分钟
> **Cook time:** 35 min | **烹饪时间：** 35 分钟

## Ingredients / 食材

| Amount | Ingredient | 用量 | 食材 |
|--------|-----------|------|------|
| 4 large | yellow onions, thinly sliced | 4 个大 | 黄洋葱，切薄片 |
| 3 tbsp | unsalted butter | 3 汤匙 | 无盐黄油 |
| 1 tbsp | olive oil | 1 汤匙 | 橄榄油 |
| 3 cloves | garlic, minced | 3 瓣 | 大蒜，切末 |
| ½ cup (120 ml) | dry white wine | 120 毫升 | 干白葡萄酒 |
| 4 cups (1 L) | beef broth | 1 升 | 牛肉高汤 |
| 1 tsp | Worcestershire sauce | 1 茶匙 | 伍斯特沙司 |
| 1 tsp | dried thyme | 1 茶匙 | 干百里香 |
| 1 | bay leaf | 1 片 | 香叶 |
| to taste | salt | 适量 | 盐 |
| to taste | black pepper | 适量 | 黑胡椒 |
| 4 slices | baguette or crusty bread | 4 片 | 法棍或厚切面包 |
| 1½ cups (150 g) | Gruyère or Swiss cheese, shredded | 150 克 | 格吕耶尔奶酪或瑞士奶酪，磨碎 |

## Instructions / 步骤

### English

1. Melt butter with olive oil in a large heavy-bottomed pot over medium heat. Add the sliced onions and a pinch of salt. Stir to coat.
2. Cook the onions, stirring every 3–4 minutes, for 25–30 minutes until deeply golden and caramelized. If the onions start to stick, add a splash of water and scrape up the bits — this adds flavor.
3. Add garlic and thyme, stir for 1 minute. Pour in the white wine and simmer for 2 minutes until mostly evaporated.
4. Add the beef broth, Worcestershire sauce, and bay leaf. Bring to a simmer and cook for 5 minutes. Remove the bay leaf. Taste and adjust seasoning with salt and pepper.
5. While the soup simmers, place the bread slices on a baking sheet and broil for 1–2 minutes per side until toasted.
6. Ladle the soup into oven-safe bowls. Float a bread slice on top of each bowl and cover generously with shredded cheese.
7. Place bowls on a baking sheet and broil for 2–3 minutes until the cheese is melted, bubbly, and golden. Serve immediately.

### 中文

1. 中火将黄油和橄榄油在厚底锅中融化，放入洋葱片并撒少许盐，翻拌均匀。
2. 每隔 3～4 分钟翻拌一次，将洋葱炒 25～30 分钟，直至变为深金黄色且充分焦糖化。如洋葱开始粘锅，加少许水刮起锅底焦香物，这会增加风味。
3. 加入蒜末和干百里香，翻炒 1 分钟。倒入白葡萄酒，煮 2 分钟至酒精基本挥发。
4. 加入牛肉高汤、伍斯特沙司和香叶，煮沸后转小火煮 5 分钟。取出香叶，尝味并用盐和黑胡椒调整。
5. 煮汤的同时，将面包片放在烤盘上，用烤箱上火（Broil）每面烤 1～2 分钟至金黄酥脆。
6. 将汤盛入可入烤箱的碗中，每碗放上一片烤面包，再铺上厚厚一层奶酪碎。
7. 将碗放在烤盘上，用上火烤 2～3 分钟至奶酪融化、冒泡且表面金黄。立即上桌。

## Notes / 备注

- Patience is everything for the onions — rushing the caramelization over high heat gives bitter, uneven results. Low and slow is the French way.
- 洋葱的焦糖化需要耐心——大火急炒会让洋葱变苦且上色不均。小火慢炒才是法式做法的精髓。
- No Gruyère? Mozzarella or provolone work well; Swiss cheese from the supermarket deli is the easiest substitute.
- 没有格吕耶尔奶酪？马苏里拉或普罗沃龙奶酪同样好用；超市熟食区的瑞士奶酪是最便捷的替代品。
- No oven-safe bowls? Melt the cheese on the bread toasts separately under the broiler, then float on top of the soup.
- 没有可入烤箱的碗？可将奶酪铺在烤面包片上单独用上火烤融，再放入汤碗中。
- Beef broth is key for depth — chicken broth works in a pinch but the flavor will be lighter.
- 牛肉高汤是这道汤醇厚风味的关键——用鸡汤替代可以，但整体风味会偏淡。
- Leftovers (without the bread/cheese topping) keep in the fridge for 3 days or freeze for 1 month.
- 剩余汤底（不含面包和奶酪）冷藏可保存 3 天，或冷冻保存 1 个月。
